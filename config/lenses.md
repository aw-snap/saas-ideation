# Cartographer lenses (S1)

Each cartographer owns exactly one lens. Territories may overlap across lenses. Gate B dedups them, so don't police your neighbours.

## screen-work (the computer lens)
People losing hours to screen-bound workflows. That includes legacy desktop apps and government portals with no API, unmodernized vertical software, IT and security chores in tiny organizations, and software bought or used by AI agents. The question to ask: who retypes, copies, clicks or checks the same thing all day, and what software is it trapped in?

## tech-unlocks
Start from capabilities of roughly the last 18 months (since about March 2025): computer-use agents, realtime voice, on-device models, video and 3D understanding, long-context reasoning, agent-to-agent protocols and payments, cheap fine-tuning, and whatever else search turns up. Work backward to who each one helps. **This cartographer also writes `config/tech_cards.md`**: 20+ cards, each with capability, maturity, cost, and an example unlock.

## overlooked
Underserved people and roles: jobs, communities and life situations that software vendors ignore because they look small, unglamorous, non-English, rural, elderly, informal, or low-margin.

## weak-signals
Things just starting to shift: new regulations and compliance deadlines, newly opened public data sources, and changes in how work gets done (new job titles, new workflows, new failure modes created by AI itself).
