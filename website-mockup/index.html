Seed 01Concrete example

Pivot: will the sofa fit?

Film your route from the street to the room on your phone. You get a green, amber or red verdict on whether the sofa will fit, plus a plan for how to carry it in.

Who it helps
Online furniture and appliance retailers, white-glove delivery companies and piano movers, and their customers at checkout.
Problem
When a sofa won't go up the stairs, the delivery fails. The retailer pays return freight, the walls get damaged and the sale is lost. Tape measures and online calculators can't handle real 3D routes like switchback stairs or a banister.
Why promising
It treats "will it fit?" as a robotics motion-planning problem. Once a route is scanned, a "fits my home" filter turns returns prevention into a sales tool.

Try it: check a sofa against a scanned route

Input: a 58-second route video, from the front door along the hall to the stairs.

    Seed 11Abstract example

    Context-aware phrase suggestions for AAC

    Listens to the conversation and moves your own saved phrases to the top, so you can reply with fewer taps.

    Who it helps
    People who use augmentative and alternative communication (AAC), especially with eye tracking or switch scanning, where every selection is slow.
    Problem
    Even with a bank of saved phrases, finding the right one means going through folders while the conversation moves on. By the time the reply is found, the moment has often passed.
    Why promising
    The phrases already exist; what's missing is a way to find them in time. It ranks phrases and never writes new ones, so the user's voice stays their own. The normal interface is always there, and the user can turn listening off.

    Try it: watch the phrase board re-rank

    Press the button to hear what the partner says. Then tap a phrase to reply.

    The conversation will appear here.

    Your saved phrases. Suggestions are green and move to the front.

    Seed 02

    Spotter: computer vision for the charity paddle raise

    Cameras and speech recognition log every raised paddle at the right giving level. Each pledge is saved with a 3-second clip that becomes the donor's thank-you.

    Who it helps
    Charity gala organisers, school auction committees and benefit auctioneers. Auctioneers are the sales channel.
    Problem
    Pledges are captured by hand in a fast room. Paddles get missed, numbers get misread, and reconciliation drags on for days.
    Why promising
    The paddle raise is a big money moment at a gala, and nothing records it automatically. Capturing it fixes reconciliation, and each donor's clip becomes a thank-you asset.
    Demo slot: an auctioneer clip plays "ten thousand… thank you, 214!". Paddle 214 lights up on a room grid, and the pledge lands in a running total with its clip.
