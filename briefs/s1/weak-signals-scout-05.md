# Scout brief: weak-signals-scout-05 (new roles, job titles and workflows)

Task id: s1-scout-weak-signals-05
Lens: weak-signals (see `config/lenses.md`). Read `config/context.md` first. Today is 2026-09-25.

## Objective
Find **job titles, roles and day-to-day workflows that have newly appeared or changed shape between 2024 and 2026**, especially where the new work happens on a screen and the tooling has not caught up yet. Include work whose customer or user is an AI agent. This slice is computer-centric.

## Slice
You own **how work itself is changing**: new titles in job postings (for example AI operations, agent operations, AI governance or AI compliance lead, evaluation and red-team roles, "AI trainer" data work, generative-engine optimization, fractional roles, and new titles in trades, logistics, healthcare administration and energy); workflows reorganized around AI assistants and agents (human review queues, agent supervision, prompt and workflow libraries, AI-output QA); websites, APIs and stores reorganized to serve AI agents as customers (llms.txt, agent checkout and payment protocols, MCP servers as a product surface); and non-AI shifts such as new cross-border, remote, or platform-driven workflows. Your evidence comes from job boards, practitioner communities and product changelogs.

## Questions to answer
1. Which job titles have grown sharply in postings since 2024? Give counts, growth rates or dated examples from job boards or labor-market reports.
2. What does a day in that role involve, and which screens, spreadsheets and tools does the person juggle? Quote job descriptions and practitioners.
3. Where do these new roles say their tooling is missing, improvised or held together by spreadsheets, Slack threads and copy-paste?
4. Which workflows now exist to supervise, review or hand work off to AI agents, and where are the bottlenecks (review backlogs, approvals, handoffs, audit trails)?
5. What has changed for people and businesses who now have to serve **AI agents as customers or users**: agent traffic, agent checkout, making a site or catalog agent-readable?
6. Which of these shifts reach small organizations and solo workers, not only large enterprises?

## Search angles and sources
- Job boards and labor data: LinkedIn, Indeed Hiring Lab, Lightcast, Glassdoor, Upwork and Fiverr trend reports, "fastest growing jobs" reports from 2025 and 2026, and job postings quoted verbatim.
- Practitioner communities: r/ExperiencedDevs, r/ProductManagement, r/sysadmin, r/msp, r/marketing, r/SEO, r/ecommerce, r/freelance, r/Accounting, r/paralegal, r/medicalcoding, role-specific Slack and Discord communities, and Hacker News "Ask HN" threads.
- Changelogs and platform announcements from 2025–2026 that created new work: agent-commerce and agent-payment protocols, MCP adoption, browser and computer-use agents, Shopify and Stripe agent features, and search changes (AI overviews, answer engines) that forced new marketing workflows.
- Industry and consulting reports on AI adoption inside workflows (with dates and sample sizes), and news from 2024 to 2026.

## Evidence standard
- At least **10 findings**. Each finding needs one or more of these: a verbatim quote, a number (postings, growth, salary, hours, adoption), or a date, plus a working source link.
- Include at least 3 findings from actual job postings or labor-market data, and at least 2 about serving AI agents as customers. Mark anything you could not verify `[unverified]`. Never invent quotes, numbers or URLs.
