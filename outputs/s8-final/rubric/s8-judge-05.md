# Rubric scoring — s8-judge-05

### I-1019 (Private Elder Statement Scanner)
Adjacent tools (Carefull, EverSafe, StatementLock) already monitor elder finances; on-device Gemini Nano angle differentiates but core problem is served.

### I-1050 (Built on Jev)
Vague meta-product wrapping a third-party model (Jev/Reflex-S1/System1-mcp exist as direct competitors); no concrete buyer, pain or demo of its own.

### I-1508 (Stop the Wire Before It Sends)
Eftsure, Trustpair and Stampli Billy already do vendor bank-detail verification; real BEC pain but crowded adjacent space.

### I-1555 (Same Words, More Life)
Cleanvoice, Descript and ElevenLabs already do filler removal/voice restyling; sync-preserving expressive restyle is the only twist.

### I-2043 (Client Quote Estimator for Dev Shops)
Devtimate, jira-ticket-estimator and Scope already estimate tickets; repo-grounded quoting is an incremental improvement, not new territory.

### I-2053 (Summary Reweigh Desk)
Hesper AI, Decerto ClaimsAI and GPTZero overlap but claims-adjuster liability sign-off niche and long-context whole-file check are sharper.

### I-2514 (E&O Broker's Citation Shield)
Citation checkers (Clearbrief, CaseRead, LawDroid) already exist; novel distribution wedge is bundling via insurance brokers, not the tech itself.

### I-2536 (AI PC optimiser and fixer)
Direct competitor: Microsoft is shipping an agentic Fix-it button natively in Windows 11, plus free optimizer apps — very weak defensibility.

### I-2559 (90-Day Reinstatement Filer)
Fortuna Health and Skyvern do adjacent Medicaid automation but not this narrow, high-stakes reinstatement-window filing task specifically.

### I-3046 (Lay of the Land)
Farmable, Mobble and Farm Estate GPS map farms but none capture narrated tacit knowledge with confidence-tagged provenance — genuinely fresh angle.

### I-3088 (Vendor Hold-Queue Call Agent)
Hold-skipping exists (Google Hold for Me, OsmO, DoNotPay) but full call navigation plus post-fix system-state verification for niche practice software is sharper.

### I-3529 (Screen-Side Cite Bailiff)
Westlaw Quick Check, CiteCheck AI and BriefCatch overlap heavily; the computer-use browser-driven verification and hard e-filing block is the differentiator.

### I-4005 (POA Packet Builder)
DocFills and Smart Form Filler are generic form fillers; POA-specific bank-template matching and rebuttal-letter drafting is a narrower, real niche.

### I-4501 (Screen Agent Drafts Session Notes)
Freed AI and Upheal are cloud scribes; fully offline GUI-agent typing into a no-API legacy desktop EHR is a distinct, hard-to-copy mechanism.

### I-4525 (Callback Verifier for Vendor Payments)
Trustpair and Rulebase already do voice fraud defense for payments; live outbound gpt-realtime callback is an incremental delivery mechanism.

### I-5109 (Live Interpreter With Transcript)
Direct competitors: Mabel AI, GoSpeech and Aida already offer live medical AI interpretation; transcript export is a minor differentiator.

### I-5410 (Micro-Seller Customs Declaration Autopilot)
Direct competitors: Zonos, Easyship and Tarifflo already handle customs/duty compliance for cross-border sellers; regulatory timing is the main edge.

### I-6003 (Jev: context-aware AAC phrase suggestions)
Spoken AAC, AAC Talker and Vocable already have listening/context features; ranking existing phrase bank is a modest refinement, not new ground.
