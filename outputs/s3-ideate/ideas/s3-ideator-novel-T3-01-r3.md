# Snap-and-Reconcile Toll Ledger

One-liner (≤20 words): A bookkeeper snaps photos of client toll invoices; the agent turns them into one fee-creep report per client.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Bookkeeping and accounting firms serving multiple small dental, veterinary and auto-dealer clients locked into Dentrix, Cornerstone, CDK or Reynolds.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Dentrix charges $47 per location monthly and CDK stacks setup plus $175-200 per rooftop fees that "recently increased," yet no client ever adds these scattered portal bills into one picture. (src: outputs/s3-ideate/pain/T3-dossier.md, P1, P2)

How it works (≤50 words): Each month the accountant photographs every client's toll invoices and portal billing screens with their phone, nothing typed. An extraction agent reads every photo, builds one running ledger per client across verticals, flags fee increases, and drafts a short note the accountant sends under their own name.

Why now (≤25 words): Mistral OCR 3 parses messy scanned invoices and screen photos at $2 per 1,000 pages, cheap enough for every client every month (TC-30, Dec 2025).

Demo moment (≤20 words): Photograph three mock client invoices live; the ledger updates instantly and flags one rooftop's fee jump.

Business model (≤15 words): Monthly per-client fee paid by the accounting firm, resold inside their existing bookkeeping retainer.

Business model (≤15 words): Flat fee per deal, paid by the brokerage, bundled into its standard diligence service.

# Portfolio Trial Balance Photo Feed

One-liner (≤20 words): A property accountant photographs Yardi ledger screens; the agent builds an instant trial balance across every client.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Outsourced accounting firms handling books for several small property management companies on Yardi Voyager or AppFolio with no live export.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Yardi has no self-serve public API, so data leaves only by nightly SFTP flat file, and even native reports "cannot specify the dates you would like to run reports for." (src: outputs/s3-ideate/pain/T3-dossier.md, P3, P8)

How it works (≤50 words): Each closing week the accountant photographs Yardi or AppFolio ledger screens for each managed property with their phone, no export request filed. The agent reads every photo, posts entries into one live trial balance spanning all client portfolios, and flags any account that stopped reconciling.

Why now (≤25 words): Mistral OCR 3 parses dense ledger tables from a phone photo instead of waiting on Yardi's nightly SFTP batch (TC-30, Dec 2025).

Demo moment (≤20 words): Photograph a mock Yardi ledger screen; the trial balance updates instantly, well before the nightly SFTP batch would land.

Business model (≤15 words): Monthly per-portfolio fee paid by the accounting firm, priced under one bookkeeper's hour.
