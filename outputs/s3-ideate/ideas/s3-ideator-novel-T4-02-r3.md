# Show-Once Pawn Report Bot

One-liner (≤20 words): Record yourself filing one day's pawn report once; the agent repeats it daily, right inside WhatsApp.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Pawn and secondhand-dealer owners and counter clerks required to file daily police transaction reports under state law.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Daily reports are due by noon the next day; a knowing miss is a misdemeanor up to $25,000 and jail time, yet low-paid clerks resist learning a second system to double-enter data. (src: outputs/s3-ideate/pain/T4-dossier.md)

How it works (≤50 words): The clerk screen-records themselves filing one real ticket into the police portal, narrating field-by-field over a WhatsApp voice note. The agent studies that single session, then each day messages the clerk in the same thread for that day's tickets, fills the portal, and replies with a receipt screenshot.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): Computer-use agents (TC-02) generalize a full click-and-type sequence from one observed session to new daily data reliably.

Demo moment (≤20 words): Record one filing live in WhatsApp; next day's mock tickets get filed automatically, receipt posted back in-thread.

Business model (≤15 words): Monthly per-location fee, priced under the misdemeanor fine risk removed.

# Show-Once Court Filing Coach

One-liner (≤20 words): Demonstrate one accepted filing for a court once; the agent catches every later filing's mismatches before you submit.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Solo and small-firm attorneys and paralegals e-filing the same document types repeatedly into the same county courts.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): About 10% of filings are rejected by the court, filers are billed anyway, and corrections have delayed hearings and a writ of possession by about a month. (src: outputs/s3-ideate/pain/T4-dossier.md)

How it works (≤50 words): The paralegal walks the agent through one filing the court accepted, narrating caption, format and proof-of-service quirks over a Teams thread. The agent learns that court's exact unwritten pattern, then for every new filing of the same type dropped into the same thread, checks the packet against it and replies with every mismatch before submission.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): TC-02 lets a computer-use agent generalize one accepted document's structure into a reusable check, no rulebook parsing needed.

Demo moment (≤20 words): Demo one accepted filing; drop a flawed new filing into Teams and get mismatches flagged back instantly.

Business model (≤15 words): Small per-filing fee, cheaper than one rejected-filing charge.
