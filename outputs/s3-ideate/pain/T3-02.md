# T3-02: Commercial-trade systems of record — auto dealerships, insurance agencies, property management (Half 2 of 2)

## Pain items

1. **Who:** Dealer/vendor integrator, auto dealership, CDK/Lightspeed DMS. **What hurts:** third-party API/CRM integration setup and monthly maintenance fees charged per rooftop. **Cost:** "$2000 per location for a setup fee and $175/mo per location to maintain the API"; for a 4-location dealer group, "$8000 setup and $700/mo." **Workaround:** pay the toll or drop the integration. **Quote:** "$2000 per location for a setup fee and $175/mo per location to maintain the API" — Gene Tilby, Mar 27, 2017. Source: https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/our-dms-appears-to-be-taking-advantage-of-us-what-are-you-paying-for-api-integration.5220/ (2017, older, marked). **Severity:** 4 — recurring per-location cost that scales with dealer group size.

2. **Who:** Dealer, Reynolds and Reynolds DMS, xTime scheduling integration. **What hurts:** integration fee increases without dealer control. **Cost:** "Our Reynolds integration fee for xTime recently increased to $465 per month." **Workaround:** absorb the increase. **Quote:** same as cited — rollo695, Mar 30, 2017. Source: https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/our-dms-appears-to-be-taking-advantage-of-us-what-are-you-paying-for-api-integration.5220/ (2017). **Severity:** 3 — smaller dollar figure but shows unilateral price movement.

3. **Who:** Dealer/vendor, CDK 3PA program. **What hurts:** upfront "certified interface" development fees before any monthly toll starts. **Cost:** "$30,000 upfront integration fee plus an annual renewal, plus roughly $200/mo/rooftop"; another poster cites "$10,000 (but it may be somewhere closer $40,000)." **Quote:** "I was quoted $30,000 just for integrating my DMS A/P Invoice Scanning to CDK Accounting data" — John Bergquist, Jul 31, 2017. Source: https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/cdk-third-party-access-pricing-guide.5345/ (2017). **Severity:** 5 — large one-time capital cost plus ongoing toll, described by a dealer-side poster as "How companies profit from selling your data" (yagoparamo, Jul 24, 2017, same thread).

4. **Who:** Independent dealers as a class. **What hurts:** alleged anticompetitive pricing/access practices by the two dominant DMS vendors, resolved via litigation rather than market pressure. **Cost:** CDK agreed to a $100M settlement (plus a prior $29.5M Reynolds settlement, ~$129.5M combined) covering dealers who purchased DMS services Sept 1, 2013–Aug 15, 2024; court approved fund distribution Jul 28, 2026, payments beginning Sep 8, 2026. **Workaround:** file a claim; no change to the underlying pricing structure reported. Source: https://www.dealershipclassdmssettlement.com/ and https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/closed-settlements/129-5m-dealer-management-systems-class-action-settlement/ (2024–2026). **Severity:** 5 — years-long, industry-wide, litigated harm with a nine-figure settlement.

5. **Who:** ~15,000 dealership locations (staff across sales, service, F&I) on CDK. **What hurts:** total operational shutdown after a ransomware attack; staff forced back to paper/spreadsheets for vehicle sales and service history was inaccessible. **How often:** one incident, but duration was significant. **Cost:** outage ran from June 18, 2024 with a second attack June 19, full restoration by July 4, 2024 (roughly two weeks); collective dealer cost estimated at "more than $1 billion" by Anderson Economic Group; CDK reportedly paid a $25 million ransom. **Workaround:** manual/paper deal-writing during outage. Source: https://www.blackfog.com/cdk-global-ransomware-attack/ and https://www.cloudskope.com/breaches/cdk-global-breach-2024 (2024). **Severity:** 5 — single system of record as a single point of failure for an entire industry segment for two weeks.

Source: https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/agency-management-sytems-applied-epic-vs-ams360.16321/ (undated, marked [unverified] as exact wording could not be confirmed verbatim). **Severity:** 3 — directional evidence of lock-in fear, quote wording unverified.

## Patterns

- **Per-rooftop/per-interface tolls recur across all three verticals.** Dealers pay CDK/Reynolds setup fees of roughly $2,000–$40,000 plus $175–$700+/month per location (items 1–3, 17); Yardi charges a reported per-interface annual license (item 9); Applied/Vertafore-connected tools (Zywave) are described as "excessively expensive" with hard exits (item 7). The toll is charged for access to data the customer's own business generated.
- **Consolidation and incidents produce settlement/litigation-scale harm, not incremental fixes.** The CDK antitrust settlement (~$129.5M, item 4), the June 2024 ransomware outage (~$1B collective dealer cost, two weeks of paper workflows, item 5), and the RealPage DOJ settlement with a court monitor (item 18) all show system-of-record dependence turning into major financial or operational exposure at the industry level.
- **Vendors sell "integration" as the cure for "double/triple entry," which is itself evidence the baseline product ships without it.** Items 6, 7, 10, 12, 13 show the AMS/PMS out of the box requires manual re-entry (credit card postings, cross-tool client data) that a paid add-on then claims to fix.
- **Insurance and property management evidence is thinner and more vendor-summarized than dealership evidence.** Most dealership items (1–3, 5, 16, 17) rest on direct forum quotes with named posters and dates; several insurance and PM items (6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13) rely on search-tool paraphrase rather than a verbatim quote pulled directly from the page, and are flagged [unverified] accordingly.
- **Labor-market evidence corroborates re-keying burden but doesn't quantify it.** Job postings requiring AMS360/Applied Epic/Yardi/AppFolio proficiency (items 14–15) show these systems create standing data-entry roles, but no posting in this pass stated a wage band or hours-per-record figure.
