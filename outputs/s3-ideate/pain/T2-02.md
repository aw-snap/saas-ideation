# T2-02, e-invoicing switchover: structured formats and EU mandates

## Pain items

1. **Who:** Germany, small firm/bookkeeper (any B2B recipient). **Step:** Receive. **What hurts:** Staff open the XRechnung XML, print it or export to PDF, then discard the XML — but the law requires keeping the original structured file. **How often:** Described as "the most common error" across guidance sites. **Cost:** Re-archiving risk, potential audit exposure over the 8-year retention rule. **Workaround:** Free upload-and-view tools (e.g., kostenlose-erechnung.de/auslesen). **Evidence:** "Der häufigste Fehler ist, dass die XRechnung geöffnet, ausgedruckt oder als PDF exportiert wird – die XML-Datei verschwindet dann im E-Mail-Papierkorb" (EN: "the most common mistake is that the XRechnung is opened, printed or exported as PDF — the XML file then disappears into the email trash"). Source: https://kostenlose-erechnung.de/ratgeber/e-rechnung-kleinunternehmer/ (2025). **Severity:** 4 — creates compliance risk for firms who don't even know they're doing it wrong.

2. **Who:** Germany, all firms ≥20 employees (Bitkom survey). **Step:** Receive/readiness. **What hurts:** Majority still cannot technically receive e-invoices despite the mandate being live. **How often:** Ongoing as of survey date. **Cost:** Unquantified but implies manual re-entry for the other ~55%. **Workaround:** Email/PDF intake continues informally. **Evidence:** "45%" of German companies can receive e-invoices; 96% still receive via email; sample of 1,103 firms with 20+ staff, fielded weeks 16–23 2024, published 3 Dec 2024. Source: https://www.bitkom.org/Presse/Presseinformation/Weniger-als-die-Haelfte-deutscher-Unternehmen-empfaengt-E-Rechnungen (3 Dec 2024). **Severity:** 5 — near half of mid-size firms are non-compliant with a live legal duty.

3. **Who:** Germany, Handwerk (craft trade) firms, ZDH survey. **Step:** Receive + issue, ongoing. **What hurts:** E-invoicing is experienced as added burden, not relief; volume of true e-invoices in inbound mail is only about half. **How often:** Surveyed Jan–Feb 2026, 1,926 firms across 52 chambers. **Cost:** One-time implementation cost around €3,000 per firm, plus ongoing costs. **Workaround:** None systematic; nearly half of non-issuers plan to start only in H2 2027. **Evidence:** "In 2025, respondents received an average of around 1,200 incoming invoices, but only about half of these were e-invoices... one-time implementation costs are around 3,000 euros." Source: https://www.zdh.de/presse/veroeffentlichungen/pressemitteilungen/e-rechnung-fuer-betriebe-noch-mehr-aufwand-statt-entlastung/ (2026). **Severity:** 4 — direct out-of-pocket cost with no offsetting time saving reported.

4. **Who:** Germany, accounting-software user (Zoho Books customer, small firm). **Step:** Issue. **What hurts:** Software-generated XRechnung fails the official Baden-Württemberg validator; missing bank data in the XML even though it's stored in the system; no vendor fix timeline. **How often:** Reported as recurring for this user's invoice runs. **Cost:** Unresolved at time of post; delays sending compliant invoices. **Workaround:** Manual correction or waiting on vendor patch. **Evidence:** "Die generierten XRechnungen werden vom Validator des Serviceportals Baden-Württemberg beanstandet" (EN: "the generated XRechnungen are flagged by the Baden-Württemberg service portal validator"); vendor reply cited no short-term workaround. Source: https://help.zoho.com/portal/hi/community/topic/books-generiert-keine-valide-xrechnung (undated, 2025 context). **Severity:** 3 — single-vendor bug but illustrates a class of validation failures.

5. **Who:** Belgium, KMO/SME and bookkeepers. **Step:** Receive. **What hurts:** Peppol UBL XML is machine-format only; a human-readable PDF is optional in the standard and many suppliers omit it, leaving recipients unable to read what they received. **How often:** Described as a systemic gap across the new Peppol flow, not a one-off. **Cost:** Not quantified; implies extra software/viewer needed per invoice. **Workaround:** Viewer tools; pressure on suppliers to attach PDF voluntarily. **Evidence:** "Het bijvoegen van een PDF is optioneel in de Peppol-standaard. En veel leveranciers voegen er geen toe" (EN: "attaching a PDF is optional in the Peppol standard, and many suppliers don't add one"). Source: https://www.peppol-box.be/nl/blog/peppol-belgie-problemen-2026/ (2026). **Severity:** 4 — breaks basic usability of a mandatory channel for any human review step.

6. **Who:** Belgium, KMO owners. **Step:** Platform/readiness. **What hurts:** Firms assume they are "on Peppol" without knowing if registration is actually active, and cannot always tell if sent invoices arrived. **How often:** Common misconception per advisers, ongoing since the 1 Jan 2026 go-live. **Cost:** Risk of invoices silently failing to deliver, payment delays. **Workaround:** None described; relies on accountant checking status manually. **Evidence:** "Veel kmo's gaan ervan uit dat ze 'wel op Peppol zitten', zonder precies te weten of hun registratie volledig actief is" (EN: "many SMEs assume they 'are on Peppol' without knowing exactly whether their registration is fully active"). Source: https://www.peppol-box.be/nl/blog/peppol-belgie-problemen-2026/ (2026). **Severity:** 4 — invisible failure mode with payment/cashflow consequences.

7. **Who:** Belgium, accountants/bookkeepers. **Step:** Receive, ongoing. **What hurts:** Accountants' own systems are built for automated Peppol processing; manual booking of PDFs from non-compliant suppliers costs them time, and they pass that cost on to clients. **How often:** Recurring per non-Peppol supplier invoice. **Cost:** Fee pass-through implied, not quantified numerically. **Workaround:** Accountants now require Peppol-ready software from clients. **Evidence:** "Handmatig inboeken van PDF's kost hen geld — en dat rekenen ze door" (EN: "manually booking PDFs costs them money — and they pass that on"). Source: https://www.peppol-box.be/nl/blog/peppol-belgie-problemen-2026/ (2026). **Severity:** 3 — recurring cost transferred to clients, friction in adviser relationship.

8. **Who:** Belgium, all VAT firms. **Step:** Readiness/penalties. **What hurts:** Grace period ends and FPS Finance begins fining non-compliant firms. **How often:** From April 2026 onward, ongoing enforcement. **Cost:** Fines [unverified amount not given in source]. **Workaround:** Rush onboarding before enforcement date. **Evidence:** "Vanaf april 2026 legt de FOD Financiën boetes op." Source: https://www.peppol-box.be/nl/blog/peppol-belgie-problemen-2026/ (2026). **Severity:** 4 — hard deadline with financial penalty risk for laggards.

9. **Who:** France, TPE/PME and micro-entrepreneurs. **Step:** Platform choice. **What hurts:** Firms must select and register with one of a large, still-growing list of certified "plateformes agréées" (150 registered as of 3 Sept 2026) before the receive mandate, with no single default. **How often:** One-time decision but recurring anxiety per firm; guides describe active confusion over which to pick. **Cost:** Not quantified in the source. **Workaround:** Comparison sites and accountant recommendations (Pennylane, Dougs, Abby lists). **Evidence:** "150 plateformes agréées (ex-PDP) immatriculées par la DGFiP au 3 septembre 2026." Source: https://www.pennylane.com/fr/fiches-pratiques/facture-electronique/liste-des-pdp (2026). **Severity:** 3 — decision fatigue and lock-in risk, not yet a hard failure.

10. **Who:** France, any firm without a connected plateforme agréée. **Step:** Issue/receive, post-Sept-2026. **What hurts:** Firms cannot legally send or receive invoices at all without a connected platform once the mandate is live for their tier. **How often:** Applies to every invoice once in force. **Cost:** Operational stoppage risk, not quantified. **Workaround:** None — hard legal requirement. **Evidence:** "Sans plateforme agréée raccordée, vous ne pourrez plus émettre ni recevoir vos factures." Source: https://facturation-electronique-tpe.fr/reforme/plateforme-agreee-pdp/ (2026). **Severity:** 5 — existential to invoicing/cashflow if unaddressed by deadline.

11. **Who:** France, buyer/seller pairs. **Step:** Issue. **What hurts:** E-invoices get technically rejected (format non-compliant, missing mandatory fields, SIREN/SIRET directory mismatch) or commercially refused, blocking the payment cycle until corrected. **How often:** Per-invoice risk built into the platform's automatic controls. **Cost:** Payment delay; rework per rejected invoice. **Workaround:** Read the rejection code/motif before resubmitting. **Evidence:** "Le rejet intervient automatiquement lorsque la facture ne respecte pas les contraintes techniques... Le refus... bloque le cycle de paiement et oblige le fournisseur à corriger ou à annuler le document." Source: https://www.compta-online.com/facture-electronique-codes-rejet-plateforme-agreee-ao8532 (2026). **Severity:** 4 — direct cashflow impact per incident, recurring by design of the system.

12. **Who:** France, small firms (TPE) and micro-entrepreneurs. **Step:** Issue, transitional. **What hurts:** Government had to publish simplification/tolerance measures in Sept 2025 because the original rules (e.g., line-by-line detail on international acquisitions, B2C transaction-count reporting) were seen as too burdensome for small structures. **How often:** One policy change but reflects ongoing pressure from small-firm complaints. **Cost:** Not quantified. **Workaround:** Government-granted simplifications (dropped requirements). **Evidence:** "En septembre 2025, government ministers announced several simplification measures... including the elimination of the requirement to provide line-by-line details for international acquisitions." Source: https://kpmg.com/av/fr/avocats/eclairages/2025/09/facturation-electronique-mesures-de-simplification-et-de-tolerance.html (10 Sept 2025, updated 17 Sept 2025). **Severity:** 3 — regulator itself acknowledges the original burden was too high for small firms.

13. **Who:** Spain, gestores/asesores serving autónomos and pymes. **Step:** Readiness. **What hurts:** AEAT mass communications caused "unnecessary alarm" among small firms who are not yet subject to Verifactu/e-invoicing, forcing advisers to spend time correcting client confusion about which rules apply when. **How often:** Described as a mass, ongoing confusion episode in 2025–2026. **Cost:** Adviser time to de-escalate, not quantified. **Workaround:** Professional bodies (Consejo General de Gestores Administrativos) issuing public clarifications. **Evidence:** "Los Gestores Administrativos desmontan la alarma sobre VeriFactu y factura electrónica" — the Council publicly rebutted the alarm and, mid-2026, "demanded adapting the new technological obligations to the real size of SMEs, denouncing a disproportionate model." Source: https://www.consejogestores.org/noticias/los-gestores-administrativos-desmontan-la-alarma-sobre-verifactu-y-factura-electronica/ (2026). **Severity:** 3 — mostly confusion/communication cost, but signals a poorly-timed rollout for small firms.

14. **Who:** Spain, all firms/software vendors. **Step:** Issue/platform. **What hurts:** Regulatory date-shifting — Verifactu's mandatory compliance date has already been postponed once (Real Decreto-ley 15/2025) to 1 Jan 2027 for companies / 1 July 2027 for autónomos, after software vendors had already built to earlier dates. **How often:** One postponement so far; adds planning uncertainty. **Cost:** Sunk software-development and communication cost for vendors and gestorías who prepared for the earlier date [unverified amount]. **Workaround:** None described besides waiting. **Evidence:** "the mandatory nature to these dates" was set by Real Decreto-ley 15/2025 after an earlier schedule. Source: https://www.politicafiscal.es/cartas-a-taxlandia/obligatoriedad-factura-electronica-verifactu-qr (2025/2026). **Severity:** 3 — moving deadlines create rework and mistrust rather than a one-time cost.

15. **Who:** All four countries, small-firm owners generally. **Step:** Readiness. **What hurts:** The overlap of three distinct but similarly-named obligations (structured e-invoicing, real-time reporting like Verifactu, and certified billing software) is reported as a core source of confusion even among professionals. **How often:** Persistent, cited as needing explicit disambiguation. **Cost:** Not quantified; implies misdirected compliance spend on the wrong obligation. **Workaround:** Explainer content ("guía 2025-2026") from advisers. **Evidence:** "Verifactu, electronic invoicing, and billing software systems are three different obligations with different schedules and recipients." Source: https://www.asesoratepymes.com/verifactu-vs-factura-electronica/ (2025-2026 guide). **Severity:** 3 — conceptual confusion that can lead firms to prepare for the wrong deadline.

16. **Who:** Germany, mid-size firms per DATEV commentary. **Step:** Readiness. **What hurts:** E-invoicing is reported as "not a focus" for many Mittelstand firms despite the live mandate, implying under-resourcing relative to the legal duty. **How often:** Ongoing as of publication. **Cost:** Not quantified. **Workaround:** None systematic. **Evidence:** Headline: "Datev: E-Rechnung bei vielen Mittelständlern nicht im Fokus." Source: https://handwerk.com/steuern-und-finanzen/e-rechnung-bei-vielen-mittelstaendlern-nicht-im-fokus (2024/2025, vendor-adjacent commentary — flagged as trade-press paraphrase of DATEV, treat as vendor-adjacent claim). **Severity:** 3 — indicates low prioritization risk ahead of enforcement.

17. **Who:** Germany, Steuerberater/Kanzleien. **Step:** Readiness, adviser workload. **What hurts:** Advisers must both become compliant themselves and educate/support every client through the switch, and the professional body had to issue explicit guidance that this consulting work can be billed as "Sonderarbeiten" (special work) under time-based fees — implying it is extra, previously-unbilled effort. **How often:** Per client onboarding. **Cost:** Billed hourly per the Bundessteuerberaterkammer fee guide (Zeitgebühr), amount not stated. **Workaround:** New fee category for the extra work. **Evidence:** "Nach dem Leitfaden Honorarmanagement der Bundessteuerberaterkammer können Beratungsleistungen zur Einführung der E-Rechnung als Sonderarbeiten mit der Zeitgebühr abgerechnet werden." Source: https://www.haufe.de/steuern/steuerwissen-tipps/umfrage-hoher-aufwand-bei-e-rechnung-fuer-betriebe_170_689638.html (2025/2026). **Severity:** 3 — confirms recurring, previously unbudgeted adviser labor.

## Incumbents
- **DATEV** (Germany): widely used by Steuerberater; trade press reports e-invoicing is "nicht im Fokus" for many Mittelstand clients despite the mandate, suggesting the tooling/process gap sits with client adoption more than the software itself. https://handwerk.com/steuern-und-finanzen/e-rechnung-bei-vielen-mittelstaendlern-nicht-im-fokus
- **sevDesk** (Germany): mixed Trustpilot feedback; some users report smooth e-invoice issuance while other reviews describe unrelated booking/duplication errors in the same platform, raising trust concerns during a high-stakes compliance period. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.sevdesk.de
- **Zoho Books** (Germany): community-documented case of XRechnung output failing the official Baden-Württemberg validator (missing bank data, version mismatch), with no vendor-committed fix date at time of posting. https://help.zoho.com/portal/hi/community/topic/books-generiert-keine-valide-xrechnung
- **French plateformes agréées market (Pennylane, Dougs, Abby, Kolecto, EBP, Docaposte, B2Brouter, etc.)**: fragmented field of ~150 registered platforms as of Sept 2026, each publishing comparison/selection guides for confused TPE/PME buyers — the guides themselves are evidence of decision friction. https://www.pennylane.com/fr/fiches-pratiques/facture-electronique/liste-des-pdp
- **Belgian Peppol access-point providers** (e-invoice.be, D Soft, Adminbuddy, Peppol OK, Combell): pricing ranges from free/low-volume to several hundred euros/month, with per-invoice fees from about €0.25, and basic setup costs cited at €0–500 — a fragmented, non-standard pricing landscape SMEs must navigate alone. https://e-invoice.be/blog/kosten-peppol-e-facturatie
- **AEAT / Verifactu-compliant software vendors** (Spain): vendors already sell "Verifactu-compatible" software ahead of the postponed mandatory date, which professional bodies say has outpaced actual small-firm need and caused alarm. https://www.consejogestores.org/noticias/los-gestores-administrativos-desmontan-la-alarma-sobre-verifactu-y-factura-electronica/
