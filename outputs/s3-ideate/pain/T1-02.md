# T1-02, post-service: claim status, denials, appeals, and portal access

1. **Who:** Billing/office staff at behavioral-health and medical practices. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** claims entered into the portal are not visible on the claims dashboard, or forwarded to the payer, for up to two days, so staff cannot confirm submission or begin status follow-up. **How often:** every claim batch. **Cost:** not quantified in dollars; described as routine workflow disruption. **Workaround:** wait and re-check manually; some resubmit out of uncertainty. **Evidence:** "NONE of the claims you enter today will be visible for 2 DAYS." Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, Apr 22, 2025. Severity: 3 — delays status checks but claim eventually posts.

2. **Who:** Billing staff. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** an error message ("cannot reach the payor now") leads staff to resubmit, and both submissions later process, creating duplicate claims that then generate denials/overpayment issues to unwind. **How often:** recurring, tied to portal errors. **Cost:** not stated; implies extra rework and possible recoupment. **Workaround:** manual duplicate-claim cleanup. **Evidence:** documented case of resubmitting after an error message, then finding both claims processed. Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, Apr 29, 2025. Severity: 4 — creates downstream denial/recoupment work.

3. **Who:** Practice/billing staff. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** portal undergoes "routine maintenance" weekly since February 2024, plus a 24-hour limit on search history, cutting into the window staff have to check claim status. **How often:** weekly. **Cost:** not quantified. **Workaround:** none stated; staff simply lose access during maintenance windows. **Evidence:** "routine maintenance" occurring "every single week" since February 2024. Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, Apr 29, 2025. Severity: 3 — recurring but short outages.

4. **Who:** Practice staff relying on payer data feeds inside Availity. **Portal:** Availity ("Claire" caseload data system). **What hurts:** the underlying data system was broken for 16 weeks, forcing staff to duplicate work and manually add every client's demographic info directly on claim forms instead of relying on the portal's stored data. **How often:** ongoing for 16 consecutive weeks. **Cost:** not dollarized; described as forced duplicate manual data entry per claim. **Workaround:** manual re-entry of demographics on every claim. **Evidence:** system "has been broken for 16 weeks," forcing providers to "duplicate work AND add every client demo on the claim forms." Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, Apr 22, 2025. Severity: 4 — sustained, forces manual re-entry per claim.

5. **Who:** Practice/billing staff logging into Availity. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** two-factor authentication is demanded on every single login, and unexpected logouts require clearing browser cache before re-entry is possible. **How often:** every login session. **Cost:** not stated in time, but described as a repeated blocker to daily work. **Workaround:** clearing cache, repeating 2FA. **Evidence:** two-factor authentication required "EVERY SINGLE TIME I log in." Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, Dec 21, 2024. Severity: 3 — friction on every login, not disabling.

6. **Who:** Practice staff. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** SMS-based two-factor codes fail to arrive, blocking login entirely until resolved. **How often:** intermittent, but recurring enough to be reported. **Cost:** not stated. **Workaround:** none reliable; wait or retry. **Evidence:** "When I log in it directs me to the 2-step authentication and its not sending it to my phone." Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, Feb 5, 2025. Severity: 3.

7. **Who:** Practice staff who lose access. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** a locked-out user cannot simply reset access — they are told to create an entirely new account, which then requires re-approval, likely by an administrator, before regaining portal access. **How often:** not stated as frequency, but reported as the standard remedy. **Cost:** account rebuild plus approval wait time; not dollarized. **Workaround:** creating a new account and waiting for approval. **Evidence:** must "create a new one altogether and have it approved." Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, Mar 26, 2025. Severity: 3.

8. **Who:** Billing/office staff checking denial reasons. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** payer information needed to work a denial is "never accessible," and when it is available it is "incomplete and inaccurate," forcing staff to seek the information elsewhere. **How often:** recurring, tied to denial follow-up. **Cost:** not stated. **Workaround:** calling payers directly or using other sources to verify. **Evidence:** "payer information is never accessible and when the information can be provided it is incomplete and inaccurate." Source: https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, May 27, 2025. Severity: 4 — directly undermines the core denial-research task.

9. **Who:** Practices/staff installing Availity's replacement authenticator app after SMS 2FA was retired. **Portal:** Availity (Authenticator for Availity app). **What hurts:** the mandated authenticator app that replaced SMS/voice 2FA (SMS/voice support ended Aug 12, 2025) is rated 1.0 out of 5 stars by users on the Apple App Store. **How often:** every login now requires the app. **Cost:** not stated in dollars; implies added setup and login friction practice-wide. **Workaround:** none noted besides persisting with the app. **Evidence:** App Store listing shows "7 Ratings" at a 1.0/5 average for "Authenticator for Availity." Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/authenticator-for-availity/id6751234719, accessed Sep 2026 (change effective Aug 12, 2025). Severity: 3 — forced migration with poor reception.

10. **Who:** Practice leaders/RCM staff. **General (not portal-specific).** **What hurts:** initial claim denial rates keep climbing, meaning more claims require portal-based rework, denial reading, and resubmission per billing cycle. **How often:** ongoing, year over year. **Cost:** average initial claim denial rate reached 11.8% in 2024, up from 10.2% a few years earlier. **Workaround:** none; described as a rising baseline of rework. **Evidence:** "The average initial claim denial rate across U.S. medical practices reached 11.8 percent in 2024." Source: MGMA 2024 Cost and Revenue Report, cited via https://www.physicianspractice.com/view/claim-denials-patient-collections-and-the-revenue-cycle, 2024. Severity: 5 — sets the volume of denial-portal work every practice must absorb.

11. **Who:** Medical group leaders. **General.** **What hurts:** a majority of practices report denial rates getting worse year over year, meaning the portal-based denial workload is growing, not stable. **How often:** annual trend, reported March 2024. **Cost:** not dollarized. **Workaround:** none. **Evidence:** 60% of medical group leaders reported an increase in claim denial rates in 2024 vs. 2023; 29% said denials were about the same. Source: MGMA Stat poll, Mar 5, 2024, cited via WebSearch aggregation of mgma.com content. Severity: 4.

12. **Who:** Practices submitting claim-status inquiries manually (phone/portal navigation without electronic transaction support). **General/cross-payer.** **What hurts:** manual claim-status inquiry is the most time- and cost-intensive administrative transaction tracked. **How often:** per inquiry. **Cost:** 24 minutes and about $12 per manual claim-status transaction, versus a fraction of that electronically; only 80% of medical claim-status transactions are electronic (dental plans only 28%). **Workaround:** electronic claim-status transactions where payers support them; manual portal/phone lookups otherwise. **Evidence:** "providers reported spending, on average, 24 minutes on manual claim status inquiry, costing approximately $12 per transaction." Source: CAQH 2024 Index, cited via https://uhin.org/blog/caqh-index-2023-providers/ and CAQH 2024 Index materials, 2024. Severity: 4 — quantifies the direct cost of the core claim-status task.

13. **Who:** Independent/small physician practices during and after the Change Healthcare cyberattack. **Portal/clearinghouse:** Change Healthcare-dependent claim submission, eligibility, and remittance tools. **What hurts:** even months after the February 2024 breach, most surveyed practices still could not reliably submit claims, verify eligibility, or receive electronic remittance advice, forcing manual/portal workarounds and diverting staff time. **How often:** ongoing disruption from Feb 2024 through at least April 2024 surveys. **Cost:** 78% lost revenue from claims they could not submit; 85% committed additional staff time/resources to revenue-cycle tasks; 79% still could not receive electronic remittance advice; 60% still faced eligibility-verification challenges; over half of respondents used personal funds and 31% could not make payroll. **Workaround:** manual claim submission, delayed billing, personal funds to cover payroll. **Evidence:** "78% have lost revenue from claims that they have been unable to submit"; "85% have had to commit additional staff time/resources"; "79% still cannot receive electronic remittance advice." Source: AMA survey data, https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/digital-health/change-healthcare-cyberattack, surveys conducted Apr 10 and Apr 29, 2024. Severity: 5 — systemic, practice-threatening disruption to post-service workflows.

14. **Who:** Denial management / AR follow-up specialists (job-posting evidence). **Portals:** named explicitly as NaviNet, Availity, and others. **What hurts:** the job itself is defined around navigating multiple named payer portals to investigate denials, determine appeal steps, and upload appeal documentation — described as "exhaustive research" work. **How often:** daily/ongoing caseload (specific volume not stated in postings found). **Cost:** role exists as dedicated headcount at $18-$74/hr per ZipRecruiter ranges for denial management specialists; remote AR follow-up averages $23.23/hr (Sep 2026). **Workaround:** hiring dedicated specialists or outsourcing to RCM/billing companies. **Evidence:** postings require "experience accessing payer portals such as Navinet, Availity, etc. to obtain information and upload appeals," and duties include "contacting payers to determine cause of denial and steps to appeal." Source: aggregated job postings via Indeed/ZipRecruiter/Glassdoor, accessed Sep 2026. Severity: 3 — confirms this is paid, dedicated labor, not incidental.

15. **Who:** Practices/reviewers of Availity as a multi-payer claim tool. **Portal:** Availity. **What hurts:** navigation is described as difficult and the platform "NOT user-friendly," with information users describe as inaccurate at times requiring extra verification steps. **How often:** general/ongoing usability complaint. **Cost:** not stated. **Workaround:** additional manual verification of portal data. **Evidence:** "It is very hard to navigate through Availity"; described as "NOT user-friendly!" Source: G2 reviews aggregation, https://www.g2.com/sellers/availity, accessed 2026 (review dates not individually visible in search). Severity: 3.

16. **Who:** Practices/reviewers of Waystar for denial/claims workflows. **Portal/tool:** Waystar (formerly ZirMed). **What hurts:** the interface is described as outdated and clunky, requiring excessive clicks, tabs, and navigation for basic workflows, and reports that should speed up denial review are instead time-consuming to go through. **How often:** general ongoing usability complaint. **Cost:** not stated in dollars. **Workaround:** manual persistence through the cumbersome UI. **Evidence:** "The interface is outdated, non-intuitive, and clunky — basic workflows require excessive clicks, tabs and navigation." Source: Capterra Waystar reviews, https://www.capterra.com/p/45512/ZirMed/reviews/, accessed 2026. Severity: 3.

17. **Who:** Practices affected by portal migrations. **Portal:** NaviNet → Availity Essentials. **What hurts:** payers are retiring NaviNet and forcing practices onto Availity Essentials on fixed deadlines (e.g., Horizon/Horizon NJ Health/Braven Health ended NaviNet use after May 31, 2024; Geisinger Health Plan phased out NaviNet for Availity by end of 2025), forcing practices to relearn workflows and re-register logins/credentials for each payer's timeline separately. **How often:** payer-by-payer, staggered through 2024-2025. **Cost:** not dollarized; implies re-training and re-credentialing effort per migration. **Workaround:** none; practices must migrate on each payer's schedule. **Evidence:** "After May 31, 2024, providers could no longer use NaviNet for Horizon, Braven Health or Horizon NJ Health plans." Source: https://www.horizonnjhealth.com/for-providers/news/updates-and-announcements/availity-essentialstm-your-new-provider-portal, 2024; and https://www.geisinger.org/health-plan/providers/updates/search-updates/2024/12/05/15/43/availity-announcement, Dec 2024. Severity: 3 — recurring disruption but payer-announced with lead time.

## Incumbents

- **Availity Essentials** (multi-payer claim status, eligibility, denials) — users report claims invisible for up to 2 days after entry, weekly maintenance outages, mandatory 2FA every login with SMS failures, a forced authenticator-app migration rated 1.0/5, and payer denial information described as inaccessible or inaccurate. https://availity.pissedconsumer.com/complaints/RT-P.html, https://www.g2.com/sellers/availity, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/authenticator-for-availity/id6751234719
- **NaviNet** — being actively retired/replaced by payers in favor of Availity Essentials through 2024-2025, forcing practices to re-learn and re-register on new portals payer by payer. https://www.horizonnjhealth.com/for-providers/news/updates-and-announcements/availity-essentialstm-your-new-provider-portal, https://www.geisinger.org/health-plan/providers/updates/search-updates/2024/12/05/15/43/availity-announcement
- **Waystar (formerly ZirMed)** — denial-management and claims tool criticized on Capterra as outdated, non-intuitive, and clunky, with reports that are time-consuming to review despite being marketed as a denial-workflow aid. https://www.capterra.com/p/45512/ZirMed/reviews/
- **Change Healthcare-dependent clearinghouse tools** — the Feb 2024 cyberattack knocked out claim submission, eligibility, and remittance functions for months; AMA surveys through April 2024 found the large majority of practices still degraded on these functions weeks later. https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/digital-health/change-healthcare-cyberattack
- **Manual claim-status inquiry (phone/portal fallback where electronic transactions aren't supported)** — CAQH's 2024 Index still measures this as the costliest administrative transaction per instance, at 24 minutes and roughly $12, with electronic adoption lagging especially for dental plans (28%). https://uhin.org/blog/caqh-index-2023-providers/
