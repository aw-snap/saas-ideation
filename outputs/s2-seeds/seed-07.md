# seed-07: Built on Jev

## Seed card

- **Title:** Built on Jev: products that only work when AI is near-instant and near-free
- **One-liner:** Jev is a new "System 1" AI model that is reportedly hundreds of times faster and cheaper than normal AI; build a product whose core loop only becomes possible at that speed and price.
- **Audience:** Open. Any system that would call AI constantly (on every keystroke, frame, event or log line) if inference were effectively instant and free. Buyer category for the card: B2B (systems and developers embedding a reflex layer). `[inferred]`
- **Pain:** Today's AI is too slow and too expensive to run on every event, so products batch it, sample it, or put a human in front of it. That rules out always-on, per-event, real-time uses.
- **Mechanism:** A fast "System 1" model makes intuitive, reflex-style judgements on high-volume, low-stakes, real-time decisions. It can sit inside other systems as a reflex layer, calling a slower "System 2" model only when needed. The specific product is not chosen.
- **Enabling tech:** Jev, a "System 1" model claimed to be hundreds of times faster and cheaper than normal AI. All claims about Jev are `[unverified]`; the group explicitly asks for them to be checked.
- **Business model:** Not stated. `[inferred]` gap.
- **Demo moment:** Not stated, because the product isn't chosen. Most direct reading: an AI judgement on every keystroke or frame with no perceptible lag. `[inferred]`
- **Core insight:** When inference becomes near-instant and near-free, AI can run on every event instead of being batched, sampled or gated by a human, and that unlocks products that can't exist at today's latency and price. `[inferred]` phrasing, drawn from the pain and excitement lines.
- **What excites the group:** Use cases that don't exist at today's latency and price; "System 1" as fast, intuitive judgement suited to high-volume, low-stakes, real-time decisions; a reflex layer inside other systems with a slower System 2 escalation.
- **Open questions:**
  - Group-stated: Verify that Jev exists, what it can actually do, and how fast and cheap it really is. Which product or buyer benefits most ("could be used in systems" is still a capability looking for a pain). How good System 1 quality is, and where it breaks.
  - Gaps seen: Whether Jev is publicly accessible (API, pricing, rate limits) for a 48-hour build. Whether the idea survives if Jev is unavailable, i.e. whether any sufficiently fast, cheap small model delivers the same loop. What the escalation rule to System 2 looks like. Business model.
- **Allowed moves:** improve / pivot / break down
