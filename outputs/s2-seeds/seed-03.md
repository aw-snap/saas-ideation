# seed-03: Lay of the Land

## Seed card

- **Title:** Lay of the Land: capturing a farm's unwritten map before it's lost
- **One-liner:** A retiring farmer drives the property talking into their phone, and AI turns the GPS track and audio into structured, confidence-tagged map layers that successors can view in AR.
- **Audience:** Users: family farms going through succession; also golf course superintendents, vineyard managers, large rural estates. Paying customers: succession advisors, agricultural lenders, rural real estate agents.
- **Pain:** A retiring farmer carries a map that exists nowhere else: drain tiles, the water line to the far trough, buried power cable, the paddock that floods once a decade. Succession researchers note that this tacit knowledge has to be demonstrated, not studied (source not given, `[unverified]`). Once the person is gone, finding buried drainage is slow and risky, because tile probes and trenching are time-consuming, invasive and can damage the pipes. The best existing tools are paper exercises such as Purdue's Code Red Contingency Planning Notebook (`[unverified]`).
- **Mechanism:** The farmer drives or walks with a phone in their pocket and talks ("Dad put the tile in here in '78, it runs down to the creek"). AI turns the GPS track and audio into map layers tagged with year, source and confidence. Later, a voice agent asks follow-up questions like a curious grandchild ("You mentioned a spring by the north fence. Does it run dry in summer?"). Successors point a phone at a paddock and see AR overlays ("per Grandad, 1978, medium confidence"). A shareable dig-safety map serves fencers and diggers.
- **Enabling tech:** Speech recognition; LLM extraction of structured, geotagged records from narration aligned to a GPS track; conversational voice agents; phone AR. `[inferred]` grouping; the group names "AI", "voice agent" and "AR".
- **Business model:** Lenders and agents pay because a documented farm is easier to finance and easier to sell; succession advisors are also named as paying customers. Pricing unit `[inferred]` gap.
- **Demo moment:** A successor points a phone at a paddock and sees "tile drain, per Grandad, 1978, medium confidence." `[inferred]` from the AR bullet.
- **Core insight:** A farm's most valuable map is tacit and lives in one person's head; it can only be captured by walking the land while talking, and it has financial value to lenders and buyers, not only sentimental value to heirs. `[inferred]` phrasing.
- **What excites the group:** Capture by just driving and talking; confidence tags on every fact; a voice agent that follows up like a curious grandchild; AR overlays for successors; a shareable dig-safety map; a paying buyer (lenders and agents) distinct from the user.
