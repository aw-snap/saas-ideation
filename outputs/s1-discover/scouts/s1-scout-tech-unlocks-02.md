# Scout tech-unlocks-02: agent protocols, payments and agents as customers

## Findings

1. **MCP auth tightened across three 2025 spec revisions** — March 2025 (2025-03-26) added OAuth 2.1 with Dynamic Client Registration; June 2025 (2025-06-18) made MCP servers formal OAuth Resource Servers and mandated RFC 8707 Resource Indicators; November 2025 (2025-11-25) shifted default client identity from DCR to Client ID Metadata Documents. Date: 2025-11. Cap-card: capability=standardized OAuth-based auth for MCP tool calls | first available=2025-03, matured 2025-11 | maturity=production-track, still churning | cost=free/open spec | unlock=SaaS teams building MCP servers get a real permissions model instead of ad hoc API keys. Source: https://modelcontextprotocol.info/specification/2025-11-25/changelog/

2. **A2A moved from Google project to neutral foundation** — Google announced A2A in April 2025 and donated the protocol, spec and SDKs to the Linux Foundation on 2025-06-23, forming the Agent2Agent project under the Agentic AI Foundation; AWS, Cisco, Microsoft, Salesforce, SAP and ServiceNow joined as foundational members. Date: 2025-06. Cap-card: capability=agent-to-agent discovery, messaging, task coordination | first available=2025-04 | maturity=production use reported in year one | cost=free/open spec | unlock=vendors building multi-agent systems get a vendor-neutral interop layer. Source: https://developers.googleblog.com/en/google-cloud-donates-a2a-to-linux-foundation/

3. **A2A adoption crossed 150 organizations in its first year** — "A2A Protocol Surpasses 150 Organizations, Lands in Major Cloud Platforms, and Sees Enterprise Production Use in First Year." Date: ~2026 (year-one report, exact month unconfirmed) [unverified exact date]. Cap-card: capability=agent interop | maturity=moving from demo to enterprise production | unlock=integrators no longer need custom point-to-point agent bridges. Source: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/a2a-protocol-surpasses-150-organizations-lands-in-major-cloud-platforms-and-sees-enterprise-production-use-in-first-year

4. **Google's AP2 launched with 60+ partners, payment-agnostic** — Google announced the Agent Payments Protocol on 2025-09-16 with more than sixty payments and technology partners (PayPal, Mastercard, American Express, Adyen, Coinbase, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Worldpay, Etsy). AP2 "establishes a payment-agnostic framework for users, merchants, and payments providers to transact with confidence across all types of payment methods," using signed Intent, Cart and Payment Mandates. Date: 2025-09. Cap-card: capability=cryptographically verifiable agent purchase authorization | first available=2025-09 | maturity=production partners onboarding, real transactions limited | cost=free/open protocol, fees via existing rails | unlock=merchants/PSPs can accept agent-initiated purchases with auditable consent. Source: https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/09/19/google-ai-payments-protocol-ap2/

5. **AP2 endorsements grew past 100 within weeks** — by the time PayPal and Google Cloud co-announced their Conversational Commerce Agent on 2025-10-27, AP2 endorsements had grown past one hundred organizations from the 60 at launch. Date: 2025-10. Cap-card: adoption pace >65% growth in ~6 weeks. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://ap2lab.com/en/blog/ap2-launch-2025/

6. **x402 revives HTTP 402 for machine-native micropayments** — Coinbase created and open-sourced x402 in May 2025, repurposing the unused HTTP "402 Payment Required" status code for peer-to-peer payments inside the HTTP request/response cycle; Coinbase and Cloudflare launched the x402 Foundation on 2025-09-23, with 22 launch members including AWS, Google, Microsoft, Stripe, Visa, Mastercard and Shopify. Date: 2025-05 / 2025-09. Cap-card: capability=pay-per-request stablecoin micropayments for APIs/agents | first available=2025-05 | maturity=early production, foundation-governed | cost=near-zero gas/stablecoin fees | unlock=API and content owners can charge per call without subscriptions. Source: https://blog.cloudflare.com/x402/

7. **x402 usage numbers, with a caveat on real agent share** — Coinbase reported 69,000 active agents, 165 million transactions and roughly $50 million cumulative volume on x402 by late April 2026, while Chainalysis counted over 100 million cumulative x402 transactions on Base through Q1 2026 but noted "much of the late-2025 surge was meme coin activity rather than agents buying services." Date: 2026-04 (Coinbase) / 2026-Q1 (Chainalysis). Cap-card: adoption is real but inflated by speculative token activity, not pure agent commerce. computer-centric: yes. Source: [reported via search summary, original Coinbase/Chainalysis reports unverified — treat headline numbers as unverified pending direct source check] [unverified]

8. **Stripe and OpenAI shipped the Agentic Commerce Protocol and Instant Checkout** — On 2025-09-29 Stripe and OpenAI announced Instant Checkout in ChatGPT (initially Etsy, with over a million Shopify merchants including Glossier, Vuori, Spanx, SKIMS to follow) alongside the open-source Apache 2.0 Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP), which non-Stripe merchants can also adopt with their existing payment providers. Date: 2025-09. Cap-card: capability=in-chat checkout initiated by an LLM agent | first available=2025-09 | maturity=production, live with named merchants | cost=standard card-processing fees | unlock=merchants get a direct sales channel inside chat assistants, no separate integration per agent platform. Source: https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-openai-instant-checkout

9. **Salesforce joined ACP within two weeks, signaling fast enterprise uptake** — "Salesforce Announces Support for Agentic Commerce Protocol in Collaboration with Stripe," published 2025-10-14. Date: 2025-10. Cap-card: adoption breadth beyond the original Stripe/OpenAI pair. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2025/10/14/stripe-openai-agentic-commerce-protocol-announcement/

10. **Card networks entered agent payments within a day of each other** — Mastercard launched Agent Pay on 2025-04-29 (Agentic Tokens extending the Mastercard Digital Enablement Service, binding a tokenized card to a specific agent, merchant scope and consent policy) and Visa announced Intelligent Commerce on 2025-04-30; Visa's more specific Trusted Agent Protocol followed 2025-10-14, designed "to enable safe agent-driven checkout by helping merchants distinguish between malicious bots and legitimate AI agents," backed by Microsoft, Nuvei, Shopify, Stripe and Worldpay. Both networks joined Google's AP2 in September 2025. Date: 2025-04 to 2025-10. Cap-card: capability=network-level agent authentication for card-present-equivalent agent transactions | maturity=production rollout, mainstream adoption targeted for 2026 | unlock=merchants can tell bot fraud from legitimate purchasing agents at the network layer. Source: https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/16/visa-mastercard-both-launch-agentic-ai-payments-tools/

11. **MCP registry has grown large but is messy to count** — the official MCP registry held roughly 18,849 servers (18,650 active) as of July 2026 per one analysis, but a September 2026 audit found 101,219 registry entries collapse to 31,309 distinct servers once historical versions are removed — "any count of registry entries overstates the ecosystem by 3.2x." Third-party directory mcp.so indexed 20,222 servers as of April 2026. Date: 2026-04 to 2026-09. Cap-card: capability=searchable catalog of tool servers | maturity=production infra, discovery still immature | unlock=builders can find pre-built MCP servers instead of writing tool wrappers from scratch. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://dev.to/leroy_jenkins_951c84b2838/i-counted-the-mcp-registry-101219-entries-are-31309-servers-and-23-have-no-source-repo-33a6

12. **Real, named CVEs in MCP tooling** — CVE-2025-54136 ("MCPoison," tool poisoning), CVE-2025-54135 ("CurXecute," CVSS 8.6, affecting Cursor before v1.3), and CVE-2025-6514 (mcp-remote, CVSS 9.6, OS command injection in versions 0.0.5–0.1.15) show tool poisoning and prompt injection are shipping as real CVEs, not theoretical. "Tool Poisoning Attacks... malicious instructions are embedded within MCP tool descriptions that are invisible to users but visible to AI models." Date: 2025 (CVE year, exact months unconfirmed) [unverified exact month]. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/blog-mcp-tool-poisoning-gateway-defense and https://owasp.org/www-community/attacks/MCP_Tool_Poisoning

13. **Bots/agents now outnumber humans on the web** — "Automated requests made up 57.5% of all web traffic by June 2026 according to Cloudflare Radar, up from 53% across 2025, which was already the first calendar year where machines outnumbered people." Much of it is GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Amazonbot and other crawlers. Date: 2026-06. computer-centric: yes. Source: (Cloudflare Radar, cited via https://dev.to/trismegistus/the-web-is-drowning-in-bot-traffic-and-ai-agents-are-making-it-worse-12ej) [secondary citation, original Cloudflare Radar page unverified]

14. **Rate limiting breaks under agent traffic patterns** — "An AI agent fanning out across 20 IPs sends 3 requests per IP per minute... while making 60 requests per minute in total," and "an autonomous agent might chain 10-20 sequential API calls to complete a single task, and if any call hits a rate limit, the entire agentic workflow fails." Date: 2025-2026 (exact month unconfirmed) [unverified exact date]. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://zuplo.com/blog/rate-limit-ai-agents-beyond-request-counts

15. **Cloudflare moved from default-open to default-block-and-charge for AI crawlers** — On 2025-07-01 Cloudflare began blocking AI bots by default for new customers and launched "pay-per-crawl," letting content owners charge AI crawlers for access; by 2026 this evolved into "Pay Per Use," and starting 2026-09-15 Cloudflare's default settings block "mixed-use" crawlers (blending search, agent use and training) from ad-hosting pages unless the site owner opts in. Date: 2025-07 to 2026-09. Cap-card: capability=publisher-side monetization/blocking of agent and crawler traffic | maturity=production, actively evolving | unlock=publishers get a lever to charge or block AI agents instead of losing traffic value for free. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-pay-per-crawl/ and https://fastcrw.com/blog/cloudflare-ai-crawler-block-september-2026

16. **llms.txt adoption is growing fast off a tiny base, with weak model-side support** — instances of llms.txt "went from 4,088 to 36,120 between June 2025 and May 2026, an 8.8x increase," yet "97% of llms.txt files received zero requests in May 2026" (Ahrefs, 137,000 domains) and "Google said publicly in July 2025 that it does not support llms.txt and has no plans to." Perplexity is a stated exception that retrieves the file. Date: 2025-07 to 2026-05. Cap-card: capability=machine-readable site summary for agents | maturity=demo-grade, low real-world pull | cost=free to publish | unlock=IDE agents, MCP servers and some assistants use it even though major LLM vendors mostly don't. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://ai.aeo.press/the-state-of-llms-txt-in-2026 and https://mecanik.dev/en/posts/does-llms-txt-do-anything-yet/

17. **Identity vendors now treat agents as first-class non-human identities** — Okta announced platform capabilities in April 2025 to secure AI agents as non-human identities; "Okta for AI Agents" reached general availability 2026-04-30; Agent SSO (built on the open Cross App Access standard) reached general availability in August 2026. Separately, "88% of organizations report suspected or confirmed AI agent security incidents, [yet] only 22% of organizations treat AI agents as independent, identity-bearing entities." Date: 2025-04 to 2026-08. Cap-card: capability=agent-specific SSO/identity governance | first available=2025-04, GA 2026-04/08 | maturity=production | unlock=IT/security teams in small orgs get an off-the-shelf way to govern agent access instead of sharing human credentials with bots. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://www.okta.com/newsroom/press-releases/okta-brings-first-class-identity-to-ai-agents-with-agent-sso/ and https://neuralcoretech.com/ai-agent-identity-governance-2026/

18. **Auth0 shipped first-class MCP auth** — "Auth0 provides early access to Auth for MCP, applying proven identity principles to authenticate and authorize every MCP client call as the industry converges on the Model Context Protocol as the standard for connecting products to the AI ecosystem." Date: 2025-2026 (exact month unconfirmed) [unverified exact date]. Cap-card: capability=drop-in OAuth/identity layer specifically for MCP servers | maturity=early access (demo-to-production transition) | unlock=small teams shipping an MCP server get auth without building an OAuth server themselves. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://www.okta.com/newsroom/articles/auth0-may-2026-product-innovations/

19. **MCP/agentic-protocol hiring exists but pay signals early-stage market** — "As of July 10, 2026, the average hourly pay for Model Context Protocol roles in the United States is $31.37, with most workers earning between $18.99 and $39.18 per hour," while some enterprise postings ask for "13-15 years of software engineering experience and PoC/PoV experience with MCP or similar AI protocols." Date: 2026-07. computer-centric: yes. Source: https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Model-Context-Protocol (hourly-pay figure); https://www.topgenaijobs.com/blog/mcp-tool-use-architecture-careers (seniority requirement) [both figures reported via aggregate search summary, not independently opened — treat as [unverified] pending direct page confirmation]

20. **Consumers are already open to agent-initiated purchases** — per Salesforce research cited in Stripe's ACP coverage, "48% of shoppers who already use AI for shopping are open to having an AI agent make a purchase for them." Date: 2025-09 (cited alongside ACP launch) [unverified original Salesforce report not directly opened]. computer-centric: no (consumer attitude stat, not itself a computer-centric build signal, included for context). Source: https://www.metarouter.io/post/what-is-the-openai-stripe-partnership

## Who it helps (backward map)
- Small SaaS/API teams shipping an MCP server — need turnkey auth/identity instead of building OAuth themselves — #1, #17, #18
- Merchants and PSPs adding agent checkout — need to implement ACP/AP2/Trusted Agent Protocol to capture agent-driven sales — #4, #5, #8, #9, #10, #20
- Publishers and content sites — need to decide whether to block, allow or charge AI crawlers and agents — #13, #15, #16
- API/platform operators — need rate limiting and traffic shaping that understands agent call patterns, not just IP-based limits — #14, #13
- Security/IT teams at small orgs — need to catch tool poisoning and prompt-injection-via-tool-description before it ships — #12
- Builders discovering/integrating MCP servers — need better discovery than a 31k-server, poorly deduplicated registry — #11
- Multi-agent system integrators — need standardized agent-to-agent coordination instead of custom bridges — #2, #3
