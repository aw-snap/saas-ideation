# Scout tech-unlocks-05: vision, video, 3D and document perception

## Findings

1. **Mistral OCR 3 — cheap production document parsing** — Launched with pricing of "$2 per 1,000 pages" standard API, "$1 per 1,000 pages" on batch API, claiming a "74% overall win rate over Mistral OCR 2 on forms, scanned documents, complex tables, and handwriting." Cap-card: document OCR/parsing | 2025-12 | production | $0.001-0.002/page | unlocks cheap bulk digitization for bookkeepers, back-office teams. Date: 2025-12. Source: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-ocr-3/ computer-centric: yes

2. **Mistral OCR 3 successor cadence** — Predecessor Mistral OCR (original, March 2025 era) already existed; OCR 3 is "fully backward compatible with Mistral OCR 2," showing iterative production releases roughly every few months through 2025. Cap-card: document OCR | iterative 2025 | production | sub-cent/page | continuous accuracy gains for scanned-document-heavy workflows. Date: 2025-12. Source: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-ocr-3/ computer-centric: yes

3. **Gemini 3 long-video understanding** — "Gemini 3 supports processing up to 2 million tokens per sequence, enabling long-form video analysis without truncation," and introduces "agentic video understanding" that "dynamically scan[s] video segments," cutting "token usage by up to 88% and costs by up to 66%." Cap-card: long-video QA/summarization | 2025-11 (Gemini 3 launched Nov 18, 2025) | production | token-based (reduced via agentic scanning) | unlocks review of hours-long footage (security, inspection, training video) without manual scrubbing. Date: 2025-11. Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-agentic-video-in-gemini/ computer-centric: yes

4. **SAM 3 — promptable concept segmentation/tracking** — Meta released SAM 3 "on November 19, 2025," which "given a short noun phrase such as 'yellow school bus' or an image exemplar... returns unique masks and IDs for every matching instance at once," open vocabulary, real-time video tracking. Benchmark: "SAM 3 achieves 75-80% of human performance on the new SA-CO benchmark." Open-sourced under SAM License. Cap-card: segmentation & tracking | 2025-11 | production (open weights) | free/self-hosted | unlocks shelf-audit counting, object tracking across video for retail/construction. Date: 2025-11. Source: https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/sam-3-segment-anything-with-concepts/ computer-centric: no (physical-world objects, not screens/PDFs)

5. **Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image) — image editing pricing** — "$0.15 per image for standard resolution edits. 4K outputs are charged at double the standard rate, making them $0.24 per 4K image." Predecessor "Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (Nano Banana) is $0.039 per image, or $0.0195 in batch." Cap-card: image editing/generation | Nano Banana 2025 (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image), Pro tier later 2025 | production | $0.02-$0.24/image | unlocks cheap automated photo edits/enhancement at scale. Date: [pricing page undated, aggregator accessed 2026]. Source: https://benchlm.ai/media-pricing/nano-banana [unverified — aggregator, not vendor page directly confirmed] computer-centric: no

6. **Veo 3 video generation pricing drop** — "Google's September 2025 pricing restructure brought significant changes to Veo 3's cost structure, with the standard Veo 3 model now running at $0.40 per second, down from its previous $0.75 rate... Veo 3 Fast delivers at $0.15 per second." Cap-card: video generation | Veo 3 2025 (repriced 2025-09) | production | $0.15-0.40/sec | unlocks cheaper synthetic video for marketing/training content creators. Date: 2025-09. Source: https://vidpros.com/breaking-down-the-costs-creating-1-minute-videos-with-ai-tools/ [unverified — secondary aggregator, not Google's own pricing page] computer-centric: no

7. **Sora 2 per-second API pricing** — "standard Sora 2 API pricing is approximately $0.10 per second at 720p resolution," roughly "$1 for a 10-second clip," with Sora 2 Pro "closer to $3 for a comparable 10-second clip." Cap-card: video generation | Sora 2 (2025, exact month unverified) | production API + invite-only consumer app | $0.10-$0.30/sec | unlocks low-cost short synthetic video generation. Date: [unverified exact launch month]. Source: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sora-2-pricing computer-centric: no

8. **Claims adjusters overwhelmingly reject AI review tools** — "98% of claims-adjuster reviews mentioning AI were negative—the highest criticism rate among all occupations studied," per Glassdoor analysis covering "June 2025 through May 2026." An adjuster is quoted: "AI is getting things wrong...it's implementing more work onto adjusters." Affects: claims adjusters (insurance). Date: 2026 (data covers mid-2025 to mid-2026). Source: https://www.techspot.com/news/113688-claims-adjusters-ai-making-insurance-process-harder-not.html computer-centric: no (photos of physical damage, not screens/PDFs primarily, though includes document summarization)

9. **Hallucination cascades from scanned documents in claims** — "A smudge on a document from an attorney, for example, can lead to a hallucination," and "LLMs summarizing hundreds of pages of medical records can hallucinate from a single smudged PDF, cascading into incorrect payouts." Also: "when a hallucinated medical summary produces a wrong payout, the adjuster takes the hit." Affects: claims adjusters, medical-records reviewers. Date: [article dated 2026 per TechSpot URL numbering, content references 2025-2026 period]. Source: https://www.techspot.com/news/113688-claims-adjusters-ai-making-insurance-process-harder-not.html computer-centric: yes (scanned PDF/medical record extraction)

10. **AI-driven employment drop in claims processing** — "Employment in the sector dropped 21% between May 2025 and May 2026, according to BLS data, and entry-level job postings had fallen 50% since 2025." Affects: claims adjusters/entry-level insurance ops. Date: 2025-2026. Source: https://www.techspot.com/news/113688-claims-adjusters-ai-making-insurance-process-harder-not.html [unverified — BLS figure relayed via secondary article, not the BLS source itself] computer-centric: no

11. **EU AI Act Article 50 deepfake disclosure mandate** — "Deployers of AI systems generating deepfakes" must "disclose that the content has been artificially generated or manipulated," with exemptions for "artistic, creative, satirical, or fictional works" where disclosure must not "hamper the display or enjoyment of the work." Compliance date: "August 2, 2026." Affects: anyone deploying image/video generation tools in the EU, including SaaS vendors. Date: enters into force 2026-08. Source: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/ computer-centric: no

12. **SAM 3 open-vocabulary detection scale** — SAM 3's data engine "automatically annotated over 4 million unique concepts," and its benchmark "SA-CO" contains "270K unique concepts, over 50 times more than existing benchmarks." Indicates production-scale open-vocabulary object detection now feasible off the shelf. Date: 2025-11. Source: https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/sam-3-segment-anything-with-concepts/ computer-centric: no

13. **Sora 2 consumer bundling** — OpenAI "bundles Sora access into ChatGPT subscription tiers (Pro: $200/Month)," plus "an invite/free tier for consumers," indicating a demo-to-production spread from free/invite access to metered API. Cap-card: video generation | 2025 | production (tiered) | $200/mo bundle or $0.10/sec API | unlocks consumer-level synthetic video without per-clip billing setup. Date: 2025 (exact month unverified). Source: https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sora-2-pricing computer-centric: no

14. **Verisk AI tools for property claims photo review (industry adoption signal)** — A 2025 Verisk press release announces "new AI tools to streamline the property claims experience," indicating insurers are actively deploying photo/damage-assessment AI into claims workflows industry-wide, coinciding with adjuster pushback found in finding 8. Date: 2025. Source: https://s29.q4cdn.com/767340216/files/doc_news/Verisk-Introduces-New-AI-Tools-to-Streamline-the-Property-Claims-Experience-2025.pdf [unverified — title/existence only, not fetched in full] computer-centric: no

## Who it helps (backward map)
- Bookkeepers / AP clerks keying scanned invoices — cheap production OCR at $0.001-0.002/page removes the cost barrier to automating scan-to-ledger entry — findings #1, #2
- Claims adjusters reviewing damage photos and medical-record PDFs — long-video and document AI tools are already deployed but generate hallucinated summaries that adjusters must catch and are blamed for — findings #8, #9, #10, #14
- Security/training/inspection video reviewers sitting through hours of footage — Gemini 3's agentic long-video understanding cuts the token/cost overhead of scrubbing raw footage — finding #3
- Retail shelf auditors / inventory counters and construction progress trackers — SAM 3's promptable, open-vocabulary segmentation and tracking lets non-experts count/track objects in photos and video via text prompts — findings #4, #12
- Marketing and content teams needing synthetic video/image assets — falling per-second/per-image pricing on Veo 3, Sora 2 and Nano Banana makes short synthetic clips and edited images commodity-priced — findings #5, #6, #7, #13
- Compliance/legal teams at any company shipping AI-generated images or video in the EU — must build in artificial-content disclosure ahead of the August 2026 deadline — finding #11
