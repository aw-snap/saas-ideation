# Scout: overlooked, slice 2 — unglamorous low-margin verticals on unmodernized software

## Findings

1. **Pawn transactions must be reported to police within tight windows, forcing dedicated reporting software.** Vertical: pawn shops. Evidence: California requires secondhand dealers/coin dealers to "report daily, or no later than the next business day excluding weekends and holidays after receipt or purchase of secondhand tangible property," and Washington requires pawnbrokers to "enter within 24 hours any and all requested information into the database prescribed by the law enforcement agency." Source: https://www.simmrinlawgroup.com/california-business-and-professions-code-section-21628/ and https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=19.60&full=true (current statute text, undated but active as of search). Affected: pawnshop owner/staff. Stuck software/portal: LEADS Online / BWI / RAPID police-reporting feeds bolted onto pawn POS. Computer-centric: yes.

2. **Late or missed pawn reports carry escalating criminal penalties in California.** Vertical: pawn shops. Evidence: "First-Time Offense: Up to 2 months in a county jail and court fines reaching $1,500... Third or Subsequent Offense: Up to 6 months in a county jail and administrative criminal fines up to $25,000," and a conviction "allows the local police or licensing board to suspend or revoke your secondhand dealer license." Source: https://www.cronisraelsandstark.com/ca-bpc-21628 [date on page not shown, statute-based]. Affected: pawnshop owner. Stuck software/portal: state/local pawn-reporting database entry. Computer-centric: yes.

3. **Pawn software vendors still position "PDF/email reporting to police" as a feature, implying non-integrated, manual-adjacent workflows persist.** Vertical: pawn shops. Evidence: "HI-Tech Pawn Software allows you to create PDF files for reporting, with police reports containing detailed information of the purchaser or pawner including address, scanned driver's license, and fingerprint, as well as item description." Source: https://hostmerchantservices.com/articles/pawn-shop-pos/ (2025 listing). Affected: pawnshop office staff/owner. Stuck software/portal: HI-Tech Pawn Software / LEADS Online. Computer-centric: yes.

4. **The Change Healthcare ransomware attack (Feb 21, 2024) directly disrupted independent pharmacy claims processing for weeks.** Vertical: independent pharmacies. Evidence: "Pharmacies struggled or were unable to process prescription claims, some had not received e-prescriptions for a full week, and many could not process manufacturer patient assistance copay cards. Independent pharmacies scrambled to address cash flow concerns, as payments were delayed due to this outage." Source: https://www.managedhealthcareexecutive.com/view/change-healthcare-outage-prompts-switch-in-pharmacy-claims-processing-cranks-up-tensions-between-independent-pharmacies-and-pbms (2024). Affected: pharmacist/owner, one or two staff. Stuck software/portal: Change Healthcare claims-clearinghouse dependency. Computer-centric: yes.

5. **Regulators had to intervene with emergency interim payments because independent pharmacies could not process claims normally.** Vertical: independent pharmacies. Evidence: "Maine developed an interim payment system for independent and safety net pharmacies in response to the continued disruption of the pharmacy claims system." Source: https://www11.maine.gov/dhhs/news/mainecare-announces-interim-payments-local-pharmacies-during-vendor-network-disruption-mon-03042024-1200 (March 4, 2024). Affected: independent pharmacy owner/staff. Stuck software/portal: state Medicaid claims/PBM portals. Computer-centric: yes.

6. **Independent pharmacies absorb multi-part staff-hour costs from PBM audits that require manual record reconstruction.** Vertical: independent pharmacies. Evidence: "An independent pharmacy facing a PBM audit absorbs four costs at once: the dollar demand, the staff hours spent reconstructing records, the cash-flow disruption from a mid-audit offset, and the cost of a waived appeal if deadlines are missed." Source: https://www.healthlawalliance.com/blog/what-a-pbm-audit-costs-an-independent-pharmacy (2025). Affected: pharmacist/owner or single office manager. Stuck software/portal: PBM audit/reconciliation portals, no automation named. Computer-centric: yes.

7. **Funeral-home software vendors have undergone repeated private-equity ownership churn, a pattern associated with declining support quality.** Vertical: funeral homes. Evidence: "During the last few years, many funeral home software companies have been sold to private equity companies or large corporations, and some have had repeated changes in ownership," and such buyers "may also try to cut development costs or outsource necessary things, like a quality support team." Source: https://devaims.com/blog/fdms-alternatives [vendor-authored, treat as lower-confidence; specific PE deal names unverified]. Affected: funeral director/owner, small staff. Stuck software/portal: FDMS and similar legacy funeral management systems. Computer-centric: yes.

8. **FTC's 2024 undercover phone-sweep found many funeral homes still fail to comply with pricing-disclosure rules, and the agency sent warning letters.** Vertical: funeral homes. Evidence: "FTC staffers posing as consumers made 278 calls to randomly selected funeral providers... 37 providers quoted different prices for the same services on different calls," and "On January 25, 2024, the Federal Trade Commission announced it is sending warning letters to 39 funeral homes across the country who allegedly failed to provide accurate information." Source: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/11/ftc-staff-issues-report-undercover-funeral-rule-phone-sweep (November 2024). Affected: funeral home owner/staff answering phones. Stuck software/portal: general itemized-price-list compliance record keeping (no specific system named) [unverified which system, if any, is used]. Computer-centric: no (phone/paper compliance, not clearly screen-based) — flagged for completeness only.

9. **Legacy on-premise insurance agency management systems (older Applied Epic and similar) still require dedicated IT infrastructure and multi-month implementations, unlike newer entrants.** Vertical: small independent insurance agencies. Evidence: "Legacy platforms like older versions of Applied Epic have on-premise deployments that require IT infrastructure," and "Enterprise systems like Applied Epic and AMS360 typically require three to six months of implementation, dedicated training, and significant setup fees." Source: https://glovebox.io/blog/best-insurance-agency-management-systems/ [vendor blog, 2025-dated content]. Affected: agency owner/office manager. Stuck software/portal: Applied Epic, AMS360 (Vertafore). Computer-centric: yes.

10. **A small agency reported Zywave withholding their own client data for weeks after a platform dispute, and unannounced ~45% price hikes hit switching agencies.** Vertical: small independent insurance agencies. Evidence: "Zywave has effectively held a large portion of our agency client data hostage for almost 2 months which is a terrible business practice," and separately, agencies that "switched from Insurance Pro to Agency Matrix... found it lacked necessary features with a sharp increase of 45% in costs this year between AM and ITC with no warning." Source: https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/problems-with-zywave.107556/ and search-aggregated Capterra/G2 review text [exact review URL for the 45% figure not directly opened — mark unverified for that specific claim]. Affected: agency owner/office manager. Stuck software/portal: Zywave / Agency Matrix / Insurance Pro. Computer-centric: yes.

11. **The 2021 Zywave/ITC breach exposed sensitive data for millions of insurance customers tied to small agencies using its AMS.** Vertical: small independent insurance agencies. Evidence: "Hackers breached Zywave's cloud-based insurance agency management system on February 27, 2021... The breach allegedly exposed the information of more than 4 million insurance customers," including "Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, dates of birth, and log-in credentials." Note: 2021 event, older than the 2024-2026 preference window, included because it still shapes agency security posture discussed in 2024-2026 commentary. Source: https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/privacy/data-breach/zywave-data-breach-exposed-insurance-agency-customers-alleges-class-action-lawsuit/. Affected: agency owner/office manager, and their customers. Stuck software/portal: Zywave / Insurance Technologies Corp (ITC) AMS. Computer-centric: yes.

12. **The freight brokerage industry is described as running on legacy systems with manual processes even in current trade commentary.** Vertical: small freight brokers. Evidence: "the freight broker industry has been described as being 'riddled with legacy systems and manual processes'." Source: aggregated from G2/Capterra TMS review pages surfaced in search (e.g., https://www.g2.com/products/freight-genius-tms/discuss, https://capterra.com/p/146697/Teknowlogi-TAI-TMS/reviews/) [specific attributing URL for the quote not individually confirmed — mark unverified]. Affected: broker/dispatcher, small office staff. Stuck software/portal: legacy TMS with brittle EDI connections. Computer-centric: yes.

13. **A freight TMS reviewer describes daily EDI failures and inaccessible tracking records on an old system before switching.** Vertical: small freight brokers. Evidence: "One reviewer mentioned switching from an old, outdated system where EDI connections failed daily and tracking records couldn't be accessed." Source: https://capterra.com/p/146697/Teknowlogi-TAI-TMS/reviews/?page=2 (Capterra reviews page, 2025/2026-dated listing). Affected: broker/office staff. Stuck software/portal: legacy TMS (unnamed) preceding Tai TMS. Computer-centric: yes.

14. **A current Tai TMS user reports unannounced API/webhook changes breaking integrations and weak compliance features.** Vertical: small freight brokers. Evidence: "too many bugs with slow support resolution, API/webhook specs changing without notice and breaking integrations, and poor compliance consciousness." Source: https://capterra.com/p/146697/Teknowlogi-TAI-TMS/reviews/ (2025/2026). Affected: broker/dispatcher. Stuck software/portal: Tai TMS. Computer-centric: yes.

15. **Veterinary practice staff report multi-hour daily waits on charting software that doesn't talk to lab or ICU systems, forcing rekeying.** Vertical: small veterinary practices. Evidence: "it does not communicate with our lab machines, it does not communicate with our ICU treatment software, I waste literal hours staring at it waiting for it to load just so I can enter charges." Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/99976/Cornerstone-Practice-Management/reviews (Cornerstone/IDEXX reviews, 2025/2026-dated page). Affected: vet tech/office manager. Stuck software/portal: Cornerstone Practice Management (IDEXX). Computer-centric: yes.

16. **Support quality for a widely used vet practice system degraded after a corporate acquisition, per multiple reviewers.** Vertical: small veterinary practices. Evidence: "support quality degradation following the Covetrus acquisition, with multiple reviewers describing strong support in the early years that has become inconsistent, with response times stretching from same-day resolution to multi-day waits for tickets." Source: https://capterra.com/p/130107/Covetrus-Pulse/reviews/?page=2 (2025/2026). Affected: clinic owner/office manager. Stuck software/portal: Impromed / Covetrus Pulse. Computer-centric: yes.

17. **Multi-state DMV/MVR access for tow/impound yards is sold as a paid add-on module, implying the underlying default is manual, state-by-state portal lookups.** Vertical: tow and auto-salvage yards. Evidence: "VTS CLOUD's Multi-State DMV/MVR Module provides online access to 38+ state DMVs for owner, lienholder, and title information, and notification and lien letters mail via electronic certified mail." Source: https://vts-systems.com/impound-software/ [vendor site, undated]. Affected: tow yard owner/office staff. Stuck software/portal: state-by-state DMV title/lienholder portals. Computer-centric: yes.

18. **Connecticut built a dedicated online "Towing Inquiry System" specifically because tow operators needed a compliant channel for nonconsensual-tow DMV notifications**, evidence this is a manual, jurisdiction-specific compliance burden. Vertical: tow yards. Evidence: "The Connecticut DMV implemented a new Towing Inquiry System accessed through the Internet, which is a secure system developed to assist towing entities with notification requirements in connection with nonconsensual tows." Source: https://portal.ct.gov/DMV/Dealers-and-Repairs/Dealers-and-Repairs/Towing-Portal-System [undated, government page]. Affected: tow yard owner/office manager. Stuck software/portal: CT DMV Towing Portal (state-specific, non-standard across states). Computer-centric: yes.

## Territories the evidence suggests

- Mandated same-day/next-day reporting from a vertical POS into a government portal (police pawn databases, DMV/lien notification portals) with criminal penalties for lateness, done by hand.
- Small-office staff losing paid hours reconstructing records for payer/PBM audits and reconciling claims after clearinghouse outages, with no in-house IT.
- Vertical software vendors (funeral, insurance AMS, vet, freight TMS) changing hands via acquisition/private equity, followed by degraded support, price hikes, or data lock-in that small operators cannot easily escape.
- Practice/office software that fails to interoperate with adjacent systems (lab machines, ICU devices, EDI/carrier feeds), forcing staff to rekey data between screens during the workday.
- Compliance and price-transparency rules (FTC Funeral Rule, state pawn/secondhand codes) enforced against small single-location businesses with no dedicated compliance staff.
