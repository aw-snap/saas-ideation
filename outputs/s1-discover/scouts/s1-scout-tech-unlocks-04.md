# Scout tech-unlocks-04: realtime voice and speech

## Findings

1. **gpt-realtime GA and price cut** — OpenAI's Realtime API reached general availability in August 2025 with the gpt-realtime model, improving audio quality, instruction-following and function calling. Pricing: "$32 per 1M audio input tokens ($0.40 for cached input tokens) and $64 per 1M audio output tokens," a 20% cut vs. gpt-4o-realtime-preview, working out to roughly $0.05/conversation minute. Date: 2025-08. Cap-card: capability=speech-to-speech model API | first available=2025-08 | maturity=production | cost=~$0.05/min | unlock=teams building phone/voice agents without stitching ASR+LLM+TTS. Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-realtime/ (title confirmed via search; content blocked by 403, so pricing figure sourced via https://futureagi.com/llm-cost-calculator/openai/gpt-realtime/) computer-centric: no

2. **Gemini Live native audio latency** — "Gemini 2.0 Flash Live achieves a 100ms to 200ms model processing latency from native audio tokenization running on Google TPUs," with real-world round-trip latency "consistently between 120ms and 180ms in US-based testing." Date: 2025 (review dated 2026 references 2025-era model). Cap-card: capability=low-latency native-audio speech-to-speech | first available=2025 (Flash Live) | maturity=production | cost=~$3/1M audio-video input, $12/1M audio output tokens (2.5 Flash native audio) | unlock=real-time voice apps needing sub-200ms responses. Source: https://www.autointerviewai.com/blog/google-gemini-live-voice-to-voice-review-2026 [unverified: exact publish date not confirmed] computer-centric: no

3. **Gemini Live 30 voices, 24 languages** — "Gemini Live API native audio provides richer, more natural voice interactions with 30 HD voices in 24 languages." Date: 2025. Cap-card: capability=multilingual native TTS/voice | first available=2025 | maturity=production | cost=see #2 | unlock=multilingual phone support and translation-adjacent voice agents. Source: https://www.gend.co/blog/enhanced-gemini-models-boost-voice-interactions [unverified: exact date] computer-centric: no

4. **ElevenLabs Conversational AI price cut to $0.08-0.10/min** — Published 2025-02-11: "Calls now start at 10 cents per minute" on Creator/Pro plans, "8 cents per minute" on annual Business plans, excluding LLM cost (which ElevenLabs was subsidizing at the time). The product (Conversational AI 2.0, now branded ElevenAgents) launched February 2025. Date: 2025-02. Cap-card: capability=voice agent orchestration platform (ASR+LLM+TTS+telephony) | first available=2025-02 | maturity=production | cost=$0.08-0.10/min + LLM | unlock=small teams building phone agents without owning the full voice stack. Source: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/we-cut-our-pricing-for-conversational-ai computer-centric: no

5. **ElevenLabs v3 Conversational TTS pricing** — "v3 Conversational at $0.05 per 1,000 characters (as of September 2026)." Date: 2026-09 (pricing snapshot). Cap-card: capability=expressive conversational TTS | first available=[unverified, v3 launch date not confirmed in this pass] | maturity=production | cost=$0.05/1k chars | unlock=voice cloning/branded-voice IVR and dictation readback. Source: https://www.cekura.ai/blogs/elevenlabs-pricing [unverified: aggregator, not primary vendor page] computer-centric: no

6. **Kyutai open-source low-latency STT** — "Kyutai offers two models: kyutai/stt-1b-en_fr with a 500ms delay, and kyutai/stt-2.6b-en with a 2.5-second delay," the 1B model built for English/French real-time transcription with a built-in voice activity detector. Date: 2025 (per model cards referenced). Cap-card: capability=open-weight low-latency ASR | first available=2025 | maturity=production-capable (open weights, self-host) | cost=free/self-hosted (compute only) | unlock=teams needing on-prem/low-cost transcription without per-minute vendor fees. Source: https://kyutai.org/stt/ [unverified: exact release month not directly confirmed] computer-centric: no

7. **Mistral Voxtral Realtime — 200ms open-weight transcription** — "Voxtral Realtime sustains 200 ms end-to-end delay," available in "a 24B variant for production-scale applications and a 3B variant for local and edge deployments," with configurable transcription delay "in 80ms steps from 80ms to 1200ms... Mistral recommends 480ms as the sweet spot." Batch version released July 2025. Date: 2025-07 (batch); realtime variant reported in a 2026-03 Forbes piece. Cap-card: capability=open real-time transcription | first available=2025-07 (batch), realtime variant by 2026-03 | maturity=production | cost=open-weight, self-host | unlock=on-device/edge dictation for field workers with no per-call vendor cost. Source: https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral/ and https://www.forbes.com/sites/ronschmelzer/2026/03/26/mistral-releases-open-weight-voice-ai-built-for-speed/ computer-centric: no

8. **Dental clinic missed-call cost quantified** — "Every missed call costs your dental practice $3,247 in lost revenue," and a separate source claims practices lose "$140K/Year" to missed calls; when nobody answers, "they don't always leave a voicemail. They just call the next clinic on Google." Date: 2025-2026 (blog network, exact dates unconfirmed). Who: dental front desks / small clinic owners. Source: https://controxai.com/blog/dental-clinic-missed-call-solution [unverified: vendor marketing copy, no primary data citation found] computer-centric: no

9. **AI receptionist deployment results claimed** — "An AI voice agent deployed in a dental clinic reduced front desk workload by 35% and cut missed appointments by 28%." Separate case claims "reduced missed calls by 93%." Date: 2025-2026. Who: dental/clinic staff. Source: https://www.builtwithagents.ai/strategy/dental-clinic-ai-receptionist-35-percent-workload-reduction and https://medium.com/@theo/how-one-dental-clinic-reduced-missed-calls-by-93-using-an-ai-voice-receptionist-3030bb04af9b [unverified: vendor case-study framing, single-clinic anecdote, methodology not shown] computer-centric: no

10. **Barge-in / interruption failures reported by builders** — "Most voice AI breaks when users interrupt mid-sentence or pause to think—the bot either talks over them or cuts them off. In production, agents keep talking for 2-3 seconds after an interrupt, causing overlapping audio." User complaint quoted: "the ai voice and the caller are always talking over each other, and the ai sometimes cuts off the caller," and the bot "isn't accounting for natural pauses during speech." Date: [unverified, exact publish date not shown, dev.to/product-feedback content]. Who: developers deploying voice agents (Vapi/GoHighLevel-adjacent builders) and end callers. Source: https://dev.to/callstacktech/how-to-prioritize-naturalness-in-voice-ai-implement-vad-ibo and https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/voice-ai/p/stop-ai-voice-from-interrupting computer-centric: no

11. **VAD (voice activity detection) as the fix builders reach for** — "Voice Activity Detection (VAD) solves interruption issues by detecting speech boundaries in real-time, enabling natural turn-taking and barge-in handling," and some platforms added an "Interruption Sensitivity" setting. Date: [unverified]. Source: https://dev.to/callstacktech/how-to-prioritize-naturalness-in-voice-ai-implement-vad-ibo computer-centric: no

12. **Dental AI vendors emphasize compliance and setup speed** — A vendor states its product handles patient enquiries "with full HIPAA compliance and setup in under 48 hours," and lists purpose-built EHR-syncing AI front-desk products (e.g., "Adit launched its AI Front Desk Agent"). Date: 2025-2026. Who: dental practice owners evaluating vendors. Source: https://www.controxai.com/blog/ai-voice-agent-dental-clinics-complete-guide and https://www.cloudtalk.io/blog/best-dental-ai-virtual-receptionist-tools/ [unverified: marketing copy] computer-centric: no

13. **Mistral positions Voxtral against latency-cost tradeoff of closed APIs** — Voxtral is described as "open-weight voice AI built for speed," an open alternative pitched directly against proprietary low-latency APIs, released as two model types: "a real-time model for word-by-word transcription, and a batch model released in July 2025." Date: 2025-07 batch / 2026-03 coverage. Source: https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral/, https://www.forbes.com/sites/ronschmelzer/2026/03/26/mistral-releases-open-weight-voice-ai-built-for-speed/ computer-centric: no

14. **Voice agent platforms bill LLM usage separately from voice minutes** — ElevenLabs conversational pricing explicitly excludes "language model expenses, which the company is currently subsidizing but may charge for in the future," meaning real production cost per minute is understated by headline pricing. Date: 2025-02. Who: builders budgeting voice-agent unit economics. Source: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/we-cut-our-pricing-for-conversational-ai computer-centric: no
