# Scout: screen-work-04 — IT and security chores in tiny organizations (1-50 people, no dedicated IT)

## Findings

1. IT offboarding at small companies (5-50 person firms, per the guide's stated audience) requires disabling accounts across "email, VPN, and cloud applications," collecting devices, and wiping them before reassignment, with the guide noting the most critical steps must happen "on the day of departure." Affected: office managers/accidental admins. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025. Source: https://firsthr.app/blog/onboarding/it-offboarding-checklist

2. Cyber-insurance questionnaires for small businesses have grown sharply in length: "A few years ago, getting cyber insurance was pretty painless. You answered maybe a dozen questions... Those days are gone... questionnaires that used to take fifteen minutes now run sixty to a hundred and fifty questions." Affected: small business owners/office managers filling insurer applications. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025 (page discusses 2025 renewal cycle, references Windows 10 EOL Oct 14, 2025). Source: https://www.snl-techservices.com/post/cyber-insurance-readiness-small-business

3. The same source cites Coalition's 2024 Cyber Threat Index finding "82% of claims involved organizations without MFA properly in place," and lists small-business assessment/compliance costs of $1,750 for a full readiness assessment, $995 for platform audits, and $750-$1,000 for annual reviews. Affected: small businesses seeking cyber insurance. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025 (citing a 2024 report). Source: https://www.snl-techservices.com/post/cyber-insurance-readiness-small-business

4. CMMC 2.0 Phase 1 went live November 10, 2025, requiring Level 1/2 self-assessments as a pre-award contract condition; small defense contractors and subcontractors face "resource limitations (budget and personnel bandwidth)" and often face "early compliance pressure" from prime contractors ahead of official DoD phase dates. Phase 2 (third-party C3PAO certification) was suspended as of July 13, 2026 pending a CMMC Reform Task Force report. Affected: small DoD subcontractors. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025-2026. Source: https://madsecurity.com/madsecurity-blog/cmmc-rollout-timeline-2025-2028

5. HHS OCR's proposed HIPAA Security Rule overhaul (NPRM published January 6, 2025, comment period closed March 7, 2025) would eliminate the "required" vs. "addressable" safeguard distinction, making nearly all specifications mandatory; OCR's own regulatory impact analysis estimated "approximately $9 billion" in first-year compliance costs and "roughly $6 billion" in recurring annual costs, with industry groups warning this "would disproportionately burden small and rural providers already operating on thin margins." Affected: small healthcare practices/business associates. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025-2026 (rule not yet finalized as of the source). Source: https://www.dwt.com/blogs/privacy--security-law-blog/2025/01/hipaa-security-rule-proposed-changes-in-2025 and https://livecompliance.com/blog/2026-hipaa-security-rule-overhaul/

6. Verizon's 2025 Data Breach Investigations Report found that among breaches involving small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs), "88% ... contained a ransomware component, compared to only 39% of enterprise breaches," and the median ransom payment fell to $115,000 (down from $150,000 in the 2024 report). Affected: small/mid businesses. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025. Source: https://shieldnet360.com/resources/blog/2025-verizon-dbir-why-88-of-small-business-breaches-now-involve-ransomware-en-436

7. Sophos' State of Ransomware 2025 (survey of 3,400 IT/cybersecurity leaders across 17 countries) reports organizations of 100-250 employees had an "average recovery cost of $638,536," and cites "lack of expertise" (40.2% of victims) and "lack of people/capacity" (39.4%) as leading operational factors behind attacks — directly relevant to orgs without dedicated IT. Affected: small/mid orgs. computer-centric: yes. Date: June 2025. Source: https://greymatter.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/sophos-state-of-ransomware-2025.pdf

8. FBI/industry data on business email compromise: BEC cost US businesses "$3.046 billion in reported losses" in 2025 (up from $2.77 billion the year before); "small and mid-sized businesses account for 28% of BEC victims," and "about 45% of small businesses that experience BEC attacks go out of business within six months due to financial or reputational damage." Affected: small businesses. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025-2026 reporting on FBI IC3 data. Source: https://www.eftsure.com/statistics/business-email-compromise-statistics/

9. Google and Yahoo's bulk-sender authentication rules (effective February 2024, "enforced" as of November 2025) require SPF, DKIM, and a DMARC policy of at least "p=none" for any domain sending near "5,000 messages or more" per day to personal Gmail accounts, plus one-click unsubscribe honored within two days; non-compliant mail faces "temporary and permanent rejections." Affected: small orgs running their own email marketing/domain setup, often without dedicated IT. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2024 rule, 2025 enforcement. Source: https://www.mailgun.com/state-of-email-deliverability/chapter/yahoogle-bulk-senders/

10. G2 reviewer complaints about Microsoft Entra ID center on fragmentation and complexity: "some features are complex to configure, and important settings are spread across multiple admin portals, with confusing licensing that locks key security features behind higher-tier plans," and administration is described as spanning "PowerShell, Azure CLI, Graph API, and the Entra portal," with "slow sign-in log queries." Affected: accidental admins managing identity without a dedicated IT team. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025-2026 (current G2 reviews page). Source: https://www.g2.com/products/microsoft-entra-id/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons

11. Widely-cited Gartner research holds that password resets account for "20% to 50% of all help desk call volume" (other citations narrow this to "30-40%" as "robust across published research over the past decade" or "40% of all help desk calls"). This figure originates from older Gartner research repeatedly re-cited through 2025-2026 vendor content; treat the original figure as pre-2024. [unverified — origin year of the underlying Gartner note not confirmed in current sources]. Affected: any org handling its own password/account admin. computer-centric: yes. Date: recirculated 2025-2026. Source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/password-reset-calls-are-costing-your-org-big-money/

12. MSP benchmarking sources report "the average technician volume is reported at 50 tickets per month," alongside a shift toward automation/AI-assisted triage as routine tickets get auto-resolved, changing how Level-1 output is evaluated. computer-centric: yes. Affected: small MSPs and their technicians. Date: 2025-2026. Source: https://worldmetrics.org/msp-statistics/

13. MSP revenue-per-technician benchmarking for 2026 states the "benchmark for revenue per technician ranges from $150,000 to $200,000 for most healthy MSPs," and "those falling below $100,000 have a profitability problem" — evidence of thin per-seat margins that push MSPs toward doing the same repetitive tasks (onboarding, patching, ticket triage) across many small clients. Affected: small MSPs serving 1-50 person clients. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2026. Source: https://www.ltvplus.com/msp/msp-revenue-per-technician/

14. CMMC readiness timelines for small/midsize defense contractors are estimated at "8 to 12 months to fully prepare, implement necessary security measures, and complete the assessment process — especially for CMMC Level 2 compliance," stretching typical small-business bandwidth. Affected: small DoD subcontractors. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025-2026. Source: https://elevateconsult.com/insights/cmmc-2-0-certification-for-dod-contractors-what-you-need-to-know-before-2026-deadlines/

15. The 2025 DBIR SMB findings also show SMBs bore a disproportionate share of overall attack volume relative to enterprises, and credential abuse (22%) and vulnerability exploitation (20%) were the leading initial attack vectors industry-wide, with third-party involvement in breaches doubling to 30% — relevant to small orgs relying on many third-party SaaS vendors without dedicated security review capacity. Affected: small/mid businesses. computer-centric: yes. Date: 2025. Source: https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/verizon-dbir-smb-ransomware-attacks/

## Patterns

- **Onboarding/offboarding as a compliance-critical chore**: same-day account disabling and device wipe steps are treated as urgent and error-prone for non-IT staff (findings 1, 6, 8).
- **Paperwork inflation**: cyber-insurance questionnaires and HIPAA Security Rule proposals are both getting longer and more evidence-demanding, with real dollar cost estimates attached (findings 2, 3, 5).
- **Regulatory deadlines with phased ambiguity**: CMMC 2.0's phased rollout, suspension, and re-review creates ongoing uncertainty for small contractors with no compliance staff (findings 4, 14).
- **Admin console fragmentation**: identity/security consoles (Entra, and implicitly Google Workspace/Intune) are described by reviewers as spread across multiple portals and tools requiring specialized knowledge accidental admins lack (finding 10).
- **MSP economics under repetitive load**: thin per-seat margins and steady ticket volumes push MSPs to repeat the same admin tasks (password resets, onboarding, patching) across many small clients (findings 12, 13).
- **Incident cost pressure disproportionate to small-org resources**: ransomware, BEC and breach statistics repeatedly show SMBs hit harder in relative terms (share of ransomware, business survival odds) than enterprises (findings 6, 7, 8, 15).
