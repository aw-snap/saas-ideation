Title: Built on Jev — products that only work when AI is near-instant and near-free
One-liner: Jev is a new "System 1" AI model that is reportedly hundreds of times faster and cheaper than normal AI; build a product whose core loop only becomes possible at that speed and price.
Who it's for: Open. Any system that would call AI constantly (on every keystroke, frame, event or log line) if inference were effectively instant and free.
The pain it solves: Today's AI is too slow and too expensive to run on every event, so products batch it, sample it, or put a human in front of it. That rules out always-on, per-event, real-time uses.
What excites us about it:
- Hundreds of times faster and cheaper opens up use cases that don't exist at today's latency and price.
- "System 1" means fast, intuitive, reflex-style judgement, not slow deliberate reasoning. That fits high-volume, low-stakes, real-time decisions.
- It could sit inside other systems as a reflex layer, with a slower "System 2" model called only when needed.
What we're unsure about:
- These claims about Jev are from the group and haven't been checked yet. Verify the model exists, what it can actually do, and how fast and cheap it really is.
- Which product or buyer benefits most. "Could be used in systems" is still a capability looking for a pain.
- How good System 1 quality is, and where it breaks.
Allowed moves: improve / pivot / break down
