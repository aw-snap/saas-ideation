# Teach-Once State Registration Filer

One-liner (≤20 words): Show the agent one state's charity registration form once; it learns to fill every other state's version too.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Treasurers and executive directors of small nonprofits fundraising across multiple states who run org business through Teams.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): 38-41 states each require separate registration with no shared form since the Unified Registration Statement was abandoned; late discovery brings unlimited back-filing liability and fees up to $6,500.

How it works (≤50 words): The treasurer narrates one state's registration once on a call posted to the org's Teams channel. The agent extracts the field-mapping pattern, then for every other target state asks only the fields that differ in that same thread, fills and files each portal itself, and confirms completion inline.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): TC-02 computer-use agents replicate an observed multi-step web-form flow across structurally similar but distinct government sites.

Demo moment (≤20 words): One narrated state filing teaches the agent; it files a second state's registration live, in the same thread.

Business model (≤15 words): Per-state annual fee, undercutting existing registration-agent pricing.
