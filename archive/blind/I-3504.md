# Teach-Once Fire Report Filer

One-liner (≤20 words): An officer files one incident report on camera once; the agent fills every later report from a chat voice note.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Volunteer and combination fire departments with no records staff, reporting into the federal system that replaced NFIRS.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Crews reconstruct incidents from memory and re-enter the same address, times and unit details more than once; poor reporting data can affect federal grant funding.

How it works (≤50 words): One officer records themselves filling a real incident report once, narrating each field over the department's group chat. The agent learns that portal's field order, then after future calls any officer drops a spoken recap into the same group, and the agent drafts the filled report there for a reply-to-approve before submission.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): TC-02 replicates a demonstrated government-portal form sequence; TC-31's open streaming transcription reads the spoken recap on department hardware.

Demo moment (≤20 words): One demoed filing teaches the form; a spoken two-minute recap in chat produces a filled draft report seconds later.

Business model (≤15 words): Per-department monthly fee, scaled by yearly incident volume.
