# Show-Once Court Filing Coach

One-liner (≤20 words): Demonstrate one accepted filing for a court once; the agent catches every later filing's mismatches before you submit.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Solo and small-firm attorneys and paralegals e-filing the same document types repeatedly into the same county courts.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): About 10% of filings are rejected by the court, filers are billed anyway, and corrections have delayed hearings and a writ of possession by about a month.

How it works (≤50 words): The paralegal walks the agent through one filing the court accepted, narrating caption, format and proof-of-service quirks over a chat thread. The agent learns that court's exact unwritten pattern, then for every new filing of the same type dropped into the same thread, checks the packet against it and replies with every mismatch before submission.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): TC-02 lets a computer-use agent generalize one accepted document's structure into a reusable check, no rulebook parsing needed.

Demo moment (≤20 words): Demo one accepted filing; drop a flawed new filing into the thread and get mismatches flagged back instantly.

Business model (≤15 words): Small per-filing fee, cheaper than one rejected-filing charge.
