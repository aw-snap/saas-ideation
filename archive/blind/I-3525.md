# Read-Aloud Session Digest

One-liner (≤20 words): After each session, a local model drafts and speaks a short client summary as a voice note, never a document.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Solo therapists writing after-hours notes for clients who cannot easily read a written after-visit summary.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Therapists lose 10-20 hours a week to documentation, and a cloud scribe's default-on transcripts left one client feeling completely violated, a written summary also assumes the client reads it.

How it works (≤50 words): A local model on the therapist's laptop drafts a short, plain-language recap of what was covered and next steps, reads it aloud, and the therapist taps once to send it as a voice note in the existing client thread, with the session audio and text never leaving the machine.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): gpt-oss-20b drafts the recap and a local speech pass reads it aloud, both inside 16GB with no server call (TC-22).

Demo moment (≤20 words): End a mock session; a voice note drafts, plays back, and sends over chat in under a minute.

Business model (≤15 words): Flat monthly fee per practitioner, add-on to a local scribe subscription.
