# Show-Once Lien Notice Filer

One-liner (≤20 words): Walk the agent through one DMV lienholder lookup once; it repeats the exact steps for every future tow.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Tow yard and impound-lot owners and clerks handling non-consensual tows under state-specific notification deadlines.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): A missed lienholder notice invalidates the entire lien sale, and a voided sale can leave the tow company owing the full market value of a vehicle it already sold.

How it works (≤50 words): The clerk demonstrates one full lookup-and-notice cycle while messaging the agent in WhatsApp. The agent learns the DMV portal's exact steps and the notice format, then on every new tow messages the clerk the VIN it needs, runs the lookup itself, and posts the drafted notice for a one-tap approval before the deadline.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): TC-02's 30+ hour multi-step reliability lets one demonstrated portal sequence be replayed unattended across new cases.

Demo moment (≤20 words): One demoed lookup teaches the flow; a new mock tow gets looked up and noticed live, approved by a reply.

Business model (≤15 words): Per-vehicle fee, far cheaper than one voided lien-sale liability.
