# Vet The Vendor Before The Client

One-liner (≤20 words): Checks an AI tool's data terms against your ethics rules before you paste in a single client file.
Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Solo lawyers and therapists deciding whether a new AI tool is safe for confidential client material, with no procurement team to ask.
Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Large firms have procurement and security teams negotiate data terms; solos "often cannot," yet must independently vet every vendor contract under ABA Op. 512's due-diligence duty.
How it works (≤50 words): Given a vendor's terms of service and privacy policy, the agent checks data retention, training-use opt-outs and subprocessor lists against the practitioner's bar or licensing-board rule, flags any clause that breaks confidentiality duties, and drafts the client-consent language the rule requires before the tool is used.
Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): Long-context models read a full vendor contract and cross-check it against professional ethics rules in one pass, cheaply.
Demo moment (≤20 words): Paste a new AI tool's ToS; the agent flags the training-use clause and drafts the missing consent line.
Business model (≤15 words): Per-vendor review fee, or an unlimited monthly subscription for frequent evaluators.
