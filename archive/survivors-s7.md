# Archive survivors: S7 evolve-intake

39 new S7 cards came in from four mutators. The results:
- **7 dropped** because the prior-art hunters judged them `direct-competitor`.
- **2 merged** into existing survivors as duplicates. No duplicates were found among the S7 cards themselves.
- **30 advance**: 11 novel and 19 balanced.

No S7 card has `seed-original` or `seed-improved` lineage. The one `seed-atom-hybrid` survivor (I-5102) competed normally.

With these 30, the live pool is 156 cards: the 126 S5 survivors plus the 30 S7 survivors. No cell cap was applied at intake, because 156 is already inside the 150–200 target. Cell counts, elites and empty cells are in `archive/map.md`. Rates and flags are in `archive/stats.md`.

The role column shows only whether a card is the elite of its cell in the live pool. It is not a score.

## Merged into existing survivors (2)

A duplicate has the same buyer, the same core mechanism and the same pain. In both cases below, the existing card is the stronger one and is kept.

| kept | merged | shared buyer / mechanism / pain | why the existing card is kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| I-3093 Privileged Cite Bench | I-5304 Local Citation Existence Check | solo and small-firm litigators / check every citation in the draft against a local case-law corpus on the laptop / fabricated citations plus privilege risk | I-5304 checks only a subset of what I-3093 checks: whether each cite exists, not its holding or quote. I-3093 names a specific capability and shows the real opinion text in its demo. |
| I-1062 One-Split VAT Learner | I-5303 One-Example Split Ledger | small-firm bookkeepers / correct one mixed-rate split by hand and it becomes a reusable template / multi-tax-code invoices break extraction (the same T2 dossier pain) | Keying the template per client instead of per vendor is a variant, not a new mechanism. I-1062 names Mistral OCR 3 and has the tighter demo. |

Near-duplicates that were kept apart, because each is similar but distinct:
- **I-5302:** a union of I-2061 (drafting grounded in timestamps) and I-4501 (typing into the EHR with a screen agent). It contains each parent's core, and neither parent contains its whole mechanism.
- **I-5401:** I-3547's contract-fee audit plus a check on changed bank details, which answers a second pain, vendor fraud.
- **I-5403:** extracts incoming carrier documents, where I-1063 drafts outgoing rate confirmations.
- **I-5407:** the inbox variant of I-1053's portal walk.
- **I-5404:** the PDF-export variant of I-4051's live screen shadow.
- **I-5305:** the upload variant of I-1503's console login.
- **I-5308:** writes the DNS record itself, where I-3059 hands the owner a record to paste.
- **I-5307, I-5208 and I-5209:** each reuses a parent's mechanism for a different buyer than I-1024, I-1517 and I-4529 respectively, so none is a duplicate.

The S4 precedent was to keep interaction variants separate, and the same rule was applied here.
