# Archive map (updated at S7 evolve-intake)

The axes come from `gates/gate-B.md`. Rows are the capability bins in precedence order, and columns are the buyers.

This map shows the **live pool**: the 126 S5 survivors (`outputs/s5-reality/survivors.md`) plus the 30 S7 survivors (`archive/survivors-s7.md`), 156 cards in all. Each cell shows:
- **n**: live cards in the cell, including protected seeds.
- **a**: every card placed in the cell. That is the S4 archive plus all S7 cards, including direct-competitor drops. Merged-away cards are excluded.
- **E**: the elite, by id and name.
- **RU**: the other competing live cards.
- **seeds**: protected `seed-original` and `seed-improved` cards. They sit outside the elite/runner-up roles.

Markers:
- **(new)** is an S7 card.
- **(P)** is a seed pivot.
- **†** is an elite re-elected at this pass because the S4 elite was cut at S5.

How elites were set at this pass:
- **The S4 elite is still live:** it stays. No S7 card was judged clearly stronger.
- **The S4 elite was cut at S5:** the first surviving S4 runner-up is promoted, unless an S7 card in the cell is clearly stronger. No S7 card was. There is one exception, in B2B|extractor|balanced. I-1027 was passed over because the S7 prior-art evidence against its near-copy I-5309 (EZAppeal) applies to it too, so I-1534 was promoted instead.
