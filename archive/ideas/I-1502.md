# One Signed Form Per Vendor

One-liner (≤20 words): Tracks every AI vendor a solo tax preparer uses and keeps the required client consent current.
Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Solo CPAs, EAs and seasonal tax preparers who must get a separate signed consent for each AI vendor they use.
Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Pasting return data into personal AI without a standalone signed consent is a federal disclosure violation, fined up to $1,000 and a year in prison per instance, and a new consent is needed whenever the vendor changes. (src: outputs/s3-ideate/pain/T9-dossier.md)
How it works (≤50 words): The agent keeps a live inventory of every AI tool touching the practice's workflow, checks each against Rev. Proc. 2013-14's per-vendor consent wording, drafts the exact signed-consent form for each client relationship, and re-flags every client file the moment a vendor's terms or identity changes.
Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): Cheap long-context models can watch vendor terms and this practitioner's own client list, then draft each mandated consent form automatically.
Demo moment (≤20 words): Swap in a new AI vendor; every affected client file flips to "consent needed" within seconds on screen.
Business model (≤15 words): Flat monthly fee per practice, billed like a malpractice-insurance add-on.
