# Show-Once Pawn Report Bot

One-liner (≤20 words): Record yourself filing one day's pawn report once; the agent repeats it daily, right inside WhatsApp.

Buyer and niche (≤25 words): Pawn and secondhand-dealer owners and counter clerks required to file daily police transaction reports under state law.

Pain and evidence (≤40 words; cite the pain dossier file): Daily reports are due by noon the next day; a knowing miss is a misdemeanor up to $25,000 and jail time, yet low-paid clerks resist learning a second system to double-enter data. (src: outputs/s3-ideate/pain/T4-dossier.md)

How it works (≤50 words): The clerk screen-records themselves filing one real ticket into the police portal, narrating field-by-field over a WhatsApp voice note. The agent studies that single session, then each day messages the clerk in the same thread for that day's tickets, fills the portal, and replies with a receipt screenshot.

Why now (≤25 words; name the specific capability): Computer-use agents (TC-02) generalize a full click-and-type sequence from one observed session to new daily data reliably.

Demo moment (≤20 words): Record one filing live in WhatsApp; next day's mock tickets get filed automatically, receipt posted back in-thread.

Business model (≤15 words): Monthly per-location fee, priced under the misdemeanor fine risk removed.
